Title: It Is Established That Prayers Are Answered When the Quran Is Completed
Excerpt: "It is authentically reported from the great Successor Mujahid ibn Jabr:

"It is authentically reported from the great Successor Mujahid ibn Jabr:

"Whoever completes the Quran is granted a prayer that is not turned away." And he also said: "It has reached us that prayers are answered when the Quran is completed.

Imam al-Bukhari said: "With every completion comes an answered prayer" (Dr. Abdulaziz al-Shaya)

"There are three whose prayers Allah does not turn away: the one who remembers Allah much, the one who has been wronged, and the just ruler" (*hasan*)

And the Quran is the greatest remembrance of all (Sad. By the Qur'an containing reminder). Say "Subhan Allah" on its own, and you earn one good deed for it; but recite it from the Quran, and you earn ten good deeds for every letter, and every good deed is multiplied tenfold! (Dr. Ayman Suwayd)

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to stay up at night with Abu Bakr, talking over some matter of the Muslims' affairs. One night he stayed up with him, and I was with him. Then the Messenger of Allah ﷺ went out walking, and we went out walking with him, and there was a man standing in prayer in the mosque. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ stood listening to his recitation, and just as we were about to recognize the man, he said: "Whoever would love to recite the Quran fresh, as it was revealed, let him recite it as Ibn Umm Abd recites." He said: Then the man sat down to supplicate, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ began saying: "Ask, and it will be given to you. Ask, and it will be given to you."

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ prayed for those who learn the Quran and the hadith by heart and pass them on: that their faces be radiant, and their prayers answered.

"The Prophet ﷺ commanded his Ummah that those who were present should convey from him to those who were absent, and he prayed for those who convey with a prayer that is answered … He also said: "May Allah make radiant the face of a man who hears a hadith from us and conveys it to one who has not heard it. Many a bearer of knowledge is not himself a man of understanding, and many a bearer of knowledge carries it to one who understands it better than he. There are three things over which the heart of a Muslim harbors no rancor: sincerity of action for the sake of Allah, sincere counsel to those in authority, and holding fast to the community of the Muslims; for their supplication encompasses those behind them, . In this is a prayer from him for whoever conveys his hadith, even if he is not a man of understanding, and a prayer for whoever conveys it, even if the listener understands more than the one who conveys; because of the radiance granted to those who convey. That is why Sufyan ibn Uyaynah said: You will not find a single one of the people of hadith but that there is radiance in his face, because of the prayer of the Prophet ﷺ"

Ibn Taymiyyah
