One of the wisdoms for which Allah prescribed the rites of Hajj: the reminder of the Day of Gathering. That is why He concluded the verses of Hajj in Surah Al-Baqarah with this reason:

(And remember Allah on numbered days. Whoever hurries on in two days commits no sin, and whoever stays behind commits no sin, for the one who is mindful of Allah. So be mindful of Allah, and know that to Him you will all be gathered)

And He opened Surah Al-Hajj with it!

(O people, be mindful of your Lord; the quaking of the Hour is truly a mighty thing (1)

Al-Baqarah is a summary of Hajj, and Surah Al-Hajj is the detail. So it gave more detail about the horrors of the Gathering (On the Day you see it, every nursing woman will forget the infant she nursed, and every pregnant one will drop her burden, and you will see people as though intoxicated, though they are not intoxicated; rather, Allah's punishment is severe (2) [Al-Hajj]

And it detailed the rulings of Hajj more

And it answered the one who denies the Resurrection (O people, if you are in doubt about the Resurrection, then We created you from dust..)

Dealing with trial

(And among people is one who worships Allah on the edge: if good comes to him, he is reassured by it, and if a trial befalls him, he turns around on his face, losing this world and the Hereafter; that is the clear loss (11) [Al-Hajj]

And He mentioned an example of this turning back:

When Satan cast rhymed words onto the tongue of the Prophet ﷺ, so that He would make that a trial by which the believer is distinguished from the hypocrite; and among them was one who did not stand firm and went astray.

(Never did We send before you any messenger or prophet but that, when he recited, Satan threw something into his recitation; then Allah cancels what Satan throws in, then Allah firmly establishes His verses, and Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise (52)

So that He may make what Satan throws in a trial for those with sickness in their hearts and those whose hearts are hardened, and the wrongdoers are in deep discord (53) And so that those given knowledge may know that it is the truth from your Lord, and believe in it, and their hearts humbly submit to it; and Allah surely guides those who believe to a straight path (54) [Al-Hajj]

He mentioned the greatest cause of turning back on one's heels: hypocrisy and hardness of heart, and in the verse after it He mentioned its remedy: *ikhbāt* (humble submission), reverence, and abasing oneself before Allah the Exalted (and their hearts humbly submit to it).

And He detailed it more here:

(And for every community We appointed a rite of sacrifice, so that they may mention Allah's name over the livestock He provided them. Your God is One God, so submit to Him, and give glad tidings to the humbly submissive (34) Those whose hearts tremble when Allah is mentioned, and the patient over what has afflicted them, and the establishers of prayer, and from what We have provided them, they spend (35) And the sacrificial camels We have made among Allah's sacred symbols for you; in them is good for you. So mention Allah's name over them as they stand in rows, and when they have fallen on their sides, eat from them and feed the content poor and the one who asks. Thus We have made them subservient to you, that you may be grateful (36) [Al-Hajj]

In Surah Al-Baqarah He forbade disputing and wickedness in word and deed, insulting, cursing, reviling, ignorance, anger, and desires.

So whoever rids himself of these traits, the traits of *ikhbāt* found in Surah Al-Hajj settle in him; because emptying comes before adorning: the trembling of the heart and its reverence when Allah is mentioned. That is why it is impossible to be one who disputes without right, a habitual curser, and reverent! The two do not come together.

And whoever leaves off wickedness and disputing, Allah compensates him with light in his heart

“There is no gulp more beloved to Allah than a gulp of anger that a servant swallows; no servant swallows it for Allah's sake except that Allah fills his inside with faith”

And when you leave off disputing with your brother, He compensates you with light in the heart and an opening in the door of understanding, a fitting recompense; for the clear opening is for whoever understands the clear Book, and this is attained only through the hardship of work and biting down.

And I understand the hadith as: whoever leaves off anger, a door of understanding and wisdom is opened for him.

(And the patient over what has afflicted them): their patience and their not turning back on their heels when trials and hardships befall them (And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge..) (Al-Hajj:11)

And the establishers of prayer: throughout the Quran it is *wal-muqīmīna al-ṣalāh* (“and the establishers of prayer”), except here in Al-Hajj, where the *nūn* is dropped (*wal-muqīmī al-ṣalāh*); because the prayer is lightened for the pilgrim, so he prays it combined and shortened (and Allah knows best).

He lightened the prayer and stressed the giving for the pilgrim; that is why He said: (and from what We have provided them, they spend), keeping the *nūn*, and the other verse made clear what kind of spending it is: the sacrificial animal of Hajj and the udhiyah.

(And the sacrificial camels We have made among Allah's sacred symbols for you; in them is good for you. So mention Allah's name over them as they stand in rows, and when they have fallen on their sides, eat from them and feed the content poor and the one who asks. Thus We have made them subservient to you, that you may be grateful (36) [Al-Hajj]

Since He knew that the one who leaves off disputing, busy away from creation with the Possessor of Majesty, does not keep track of his enemies and what they weave behind his back, He frightened and terrified those people, and reassured the humbly submissive one that he should carry on in his reverence and submission and not cut it off for their sake, for the greatness of the right that is in it, for Allah is sufficient for him, so He said: (Allah surely defends those who believe; Allah does not love any treacherous, ungrateful one (38) [Al-Hajj]

I reflected on Surah Al-Baqarah and found that it began with the verses of jihad and then Hajj, while in Surah Al-Hajj He began with Hajj and then jihad.

And perhaps the wisdom is that, in Allah's prior knowledge, they would strive in jihad (the battles of Badr ,,) then there would be a conquest and Hajj, then jihad once again.

How many a town We destroyed while it was doing wrong, so it lies collapsed upon its roofs, and how many a deserted well and lofty palace (45) Have they not traveled through the land, so that they would have hearts by which to reason and ears by which to hear? For it is not the eyes that go blind, but it is the hearts within the chests that go blind (46)

There is a resemblance between the land of the Gathering and the land of Hajj! Both of them are empty of buildings

(And you will see the earth exposed, and We will gather them) meaning empty and white, with nothing in it and no building, a level land.

And likewise the lands of the sacred sites, Arafah and Muzdalifah, have no buildings on them.

And He mentioned the destroyed cities so that the pilgrim remembers what he has left behind his back of home, land, palaces, and family, and that he came to perform the rites alone, solitary, stripped bare, unclothed in an empty land, and that this is how you will be on the Day of Resurrection.

And likewise the Gathering comes after the horrors and the splitting of the heavens, the constellations, and the moons, and so He mentioned the destroyed city, an abandoned well that is of no use, and a lofty palace.

Then He mentioned traveling through the land, so that the pilgrim, in his traveling from one site of the rites to another: Mina-Arafah-Muzdalifah- Mina, remembers that creation will be traveling to Allah on the Day of Gathering like this, so let him have a heart by which to reason.
