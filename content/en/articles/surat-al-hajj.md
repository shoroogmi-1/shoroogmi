Title: Reflections on the Verses of Surah Al-Hajj
Excerpt: And when you leave off arguing with your brother, He compensates you with light in the heart and an opening in the door of understanding, a fitting recompense; for the clear opening is for whoever understands the clear Book, and this is attained only through the hardship of work and biting down.

And when you leave off arguing with your brother, He compensates you with light in the heart and an opening in the door of understanding, a fitting recompense; for the clear opening is for whoever understands the clear Book, and this is attained only through the hardship of work and biting down.

And I understand the hadith: whoever leaves off anger, a door of understanding and wisdom is opened for him.

(And the patient over what has afflicted them): their patience and their not turning back on their heels when trials and hardships befall them (And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge..) (Al-Hajj:11)

And the establishers of prayer: throughout the Quran it is *wal-muqīmīna al-ṣalāh* (“and the establishers of prayer”), except here in Al-Hajj, where the *nūn* is dropped (*wal-muqīmī al-ṣalāh*); because the prayer is lightened for the pilgrim, so he prays it combined and shortened -and Allah knows best-.

He lightened the prayer and stressed zakah for the pilgrim; that is why He said: (and from what We have provided them, they spend), keeping the *nūn*, and the other verse made clear what kind of spending it is: the hady and the udhiyah.

(And the camels and cattle We have appointed for you as among the symbols of Allah; for you therein is good. So mention the name of Allah upon them when lined up [for sacrifice]; and when they are [lifeless] on their sides, then eat from them and feed the needy and the beggar. Thus have We subjected them to you that you may be grateful. (36) [Al-Hajj]

When He knew that the one who leaves off arguing is occupied away from the creation with the Possessor of Majesty, and is not aware of his enemies and what they weave behind his back, He made those ones fear and dread, and reassured the humble one that he should carry on in his humility and submission and not cut it off for their sake, for the greatness of the right he is engaged in, for Allah is sufficient for him; so He said: (Indeed, Allah defends those who have believed. Indeed, Allah does not like everyone treacherous and ungrateful. (38) [Al-Hajj]

I reflected on Surah Al-Baqarah, and found that it began with the verses of jihad and then Hajj, while in Surah Al-Hajj He began with Hajj and then jihad.

And perhaps the wisdom is that, in Allah's prior knowledge, they would fight (the battles of Badr ,,) and then there would be a conquest and Hajj, and then jihad once again.

And how many a city did We destroy while it was committing wrong - so it is [now] fallen into ruin - and [how many] an abandoned well and [how many] a lofty palace. (45) So have they not traveled through the earth and have hearts by which to reason and ears by which to hear? For indeed, it is not eyes that are blinded, but blinded are the hearts which are within the breasts. (46)

There is a resemblance between the land of the Gathering and the land of Hajj! That both are empty of buildings

(And you will see the earth prominent, and We will gather them) meaning empty and white, with nothing in it and no building, a level land.

And likewise the lands of the sacred sites, such as Arafah and Muzdalifah, have no buildings in them.

And He mentioned the destroyed cities so that the pilgrim remembers what he left behind his back of house, land, palaces, and family, and that he came to perform the rites alone, solitary, stripped, bare, in an empty land, and that is how your state will be on the Day of Resurrection.

And likewise, the Gathering comes after the terrors and the splitting of the heavens, the constellations, and the moons; and so He mentioned the destroyed city, the abandoned well that is of no use, and the lofty palace.

Then He mentioned traveling through the land, so that the pilgrim, in his traveling from one site of the rites to another: Mina-Arafah-Muzdalifah- Mina, remembers that the creation will be traveling to Allah on the Day of Gathering just like this; so let him have a heart by which to reason.
