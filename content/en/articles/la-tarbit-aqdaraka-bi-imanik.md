Title: Don't Tie Your Fate to Your Faith

I have a piece of advice, precious and essential: don't tie what is decreed for you to your faith, or its strings will start to tremble.

Don't say, "This came to me because of that good deed," or you will become one who reminds Allah of his favors; nor, "That came because of a sin," or you will despair.

Then your faith will sway to the right and to the left: rising when circumstances are kind, crumbling when the decree is hard.

When you worship Allah, worship Him for His sake alone.

Don't tie small decrees and passing events to this great meaning of faith, or you will stumble about like a night-blind camel; your soul will be shaken, your strength blunted, and your sun will set.

Say: I do not worship You for hardship, nor for ease; I worship You only for the sake of Your Noble Face, seeking Your pleasure and Your Gardens.

And stand firm on this.

Then see the deep inner calm you will rest in.

Note: This does not deny that hard decrees may be what a servant has earned by his own hands, nor that he should call himself to account and look over his life, so that he repents and changes for the better. The point here is that faith should settle in his heart and take root there, so that he is neither insolent in times of ease nor despairing in hardship.
