Title: Don't Tie Your Fate to Your Faith
Excerpt: I have a precious, essential piece of advice: don't tie what is decreed for you to your faith, lest its strings be shaken.

I have a precious, essential piece of advice: don't tie what is decreed for you to your faith, lest its strings be shaken.

Don't say this decree came because of that good deed, and so become someone who counts his favors to Allah, or that one came because of a sin, and so despair.

Then your faith swings right and left with the circumstances: it grows strong with them, and collapses with what is decreed.

When you worship Allah, worship Him for His sake alone.

Don't tie small decrees and events to this great concept of faith, or you will stumble about like a blind camel, your soul will be shaken, your resolve blunted, and your sun will set.

Say: I do not worship You for hardship or for ease; I worship You only for the sake of Your Noble Face, seeking Your pleasure and Your Gardens.

And hold firm to this.

Then look at the great inner stability you will enjoy.

Note: This does not deny that hard decrees can be the result of a servant's own doings, and that he should hold himself to account and review himself so that he repents and changes for the better. But the point here is that faith should become firm and deep-rooted in his heart, so that he is not arrogant in times of ease, nor despairs in hardship.
