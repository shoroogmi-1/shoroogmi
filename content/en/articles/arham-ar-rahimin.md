Title: The Most Merciful of the Merciful
Excerpt: (The Most Merciful of the merciful) is linked throughout the Quran with reconciliation between brothers and bringing together those who have drifted apart.

(The Most Merciful of the merciful) is linked throughout the Quran with reconciliation between brothers and bringing together those who have drifted apart.

So it is beneficial to use this name in supplicating for reconciliation (O Most Merciful of the merciful, set things right between us)

It is the supplication of Moses (peace be upon him) (He said, "My Lord, forgive me and my brother and admit us into Your mercy, for You are the most merciful of the merciful." (151)

And the supplication of Jacob (peace be upon him) for the quarreling brothers (He said, "Should I entrust you with him except as I entrusted you with his brother before? But Allah is the best guardian, and He is the most merciful of the merciful." (64)

And the supplication of Joseph for his brothers (He said, "No blame will there be upon you today. Allah will forgive you; and He is the most merciful of the merciful." (92)

And the supplication of Job (peace be upon him) when people drew away from him

And [mention] Job, when he called to his Lord, "Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the most merciful of the merciful." (83) So We responded to him and removed what afflicted him of adversity. And We gave him [back] his family and the like thereof with them as mercy from Us and a reminder for the worshippers (84)

Difference of opinion is a mercy in jurisprudence and culture, but when it comes down onto societies and relationships, it is part of punishment and pain (Say, "He is the [one] Able to send upon you affliction from above you or from beneath your feet or to confuse you [so you become] sects and make you taste the violence of one another." Look how We diversify the verses that they might understand (65)

So these pains and torments needed a name that pours mercy onto them, so they become coolness and safety; and so it was the name (the Most Merciful of the merciful).

He is more merciful to those who quarrel than they are to themselves, so His mercy descends upon them and sets them right, and the pain turns to coolness and the rift to affection; His mercy entails His power to do that, though minds had thought it far off. And He forgives the slips and wrongs between them so that they are more able to reconcile

(Perhaps Allah will put, between you and those to whom you have been enemies among them, affection. And Allah is competent, and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful (7)
