Title: The Most Merciful of the Merciful
Excerpt: (The Most Merciful of the merciful): throughout the Quran, this name is bound up with reconciliation between brothers and with drawing together those who have grown apart.

(The Most Merciful of the merciful): throughout the Quran, this name is bound up with reconciliation between brothers and with drawing together those who have grown apart.

So it helps to call upon this name when praying for reconciliation (O Most Merciful of the merciful, set things right between us)

It is the supplication of Moses, peace be upon him ([Moses] said, "My Lord, forgive me and my brother and admit us into Your mercy, for You are the most merciful of the merciful." (151)

And the supplication of Jacob, peace be upon him, for the quarrelling brothers (He said, "Should I entrust you with him except [under coercion] as I entrusted you with his brother before? But Allah is the best guardian, and He is the most merciful of the merciful." (64)

And the supplication of Joseph for his brothers (He said, "No blame will there be upon you today. Allah will forgive you; and He is the most merciful of the merciful." (92)

And the supplication of Job, peace be upon him, when people drew away from him

And [mention] Job, when he called to his Lord, "Indeed, adversity has touched me, and you are the Most Merciful of the merciful." (83) So We responded to him and removed what afflicted him of adversity. And We gave him [back] his family and the like thereof with them as mercy from Us and a reminder for the worshippers [of Allah] (84)

Disagreement is a mercy in jurisprudence and in culture; but when it descends upon societies and relationships, it becomes part of the punishment and the pain (Say, "He is the [one] Able to send upon you affliction from above you or from beneath your feet or to confuse you [so you become] sects and make you taste the violence of one another." Look how We diversify the signs that they might understand (65)

These pains and torments needed a name that would pour mercy down upon them, so that they might become coolness and safety; and that name was (the Most Merciful of the merciful).

He is more merciful to those at odds than they are to themselves. His mercy descends upon them and mends what is between them; the pain turns to coolness, and the rift to affection. His mercy means He is able to do this, even when minds had thought it impossible; and He forgives the slips and wrongs between them, so that they are better able to make peace

(Perhaps Allah will put, between you and those to whom you have been enemies among them, affection. And Allah is competent, and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful (7)

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: (Allah has an angel appointed over whoever says: O Most Merciful of the merciful. Whoever says it three times, the angel says: The Most Merciful of the merciful has turned toward you, so ask). (weak hadith)
