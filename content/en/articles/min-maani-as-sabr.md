Title: Among the Meanings of Patience: Fasting
Excerpt: Among the meanings of patience: fasting!

Among the meanings of patience: fasting!

{O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer}

Patience here is fasting, and prayer holds the Quran within it. That is: seek help, in the affairs of this world and the next, through fasting, the Quran, and prayer.

“By patience He meant fasting. Imam Ahmad said: We have narrated this from Mujahid. This is because fasting holds patience within it: holding back by day from the food and drink one is used to, while one’s nature stirs toward them and the soul longs for them. And this is why Ramadan was called the month of patience.”

“Abu Ubayd said: It reached me that Sufyan ibn Uyaynah explained His words as referring to fasting. He said: Because fasting is patience; a person holds himself back from food, drink, and intimacy. Then he recited: {Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account}” (Shu‘ab al-Iman)

Fasting is a brightness that burns away sins and cravings; the flash of its lightning all but takes away the sight! It grants a wondrous keenness of insight, and a strength of perception and knowledge; for the Prophet ﷺ said: “and prayer is a light, … and endurance is a brightness”

That is: fasting is a brightness. Al-Bayhaqi took this hadith as proof that one of the names of fasting is patience.

And al-Asqalani said in *al-Fath*: “‘…fasting; it is for Me, and I shall reward for it’: that is, I shall reward it with a great reward, without fixing its measure. This is like the words of the Exalted: ﴿Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account﴾. End of quote. And the patient are those who fast, according to most views… I say: Abu Ubayd preceded him in this, in his *Gharib*, saying: It reached me that Ibn Uyaynah said that, and he argued for it on the grounds that fasting is patience, because the one fasting holds his soul back from its desires, and Allah the Exalted has said: ﴿Indeed, the patient will be given their reward without account﴾”

Fasting is a cure for the heart

And the proof, backed by the authentic hadith: “Whoever would be glad to have much of the rancor in his chest taken away, let him fast the month of patience and three days of every month,”

And in another wording: “Fasting three days of the month takes away the rancor of the chest”

“He says: ‘Among the things that take away much of the rancor of the chest,’ that is, that remove from it deceit and malice, or its seething anger, or its hypocrisy, or the fiercest wrath, ‘is fasting the month of patience,’ which is Ramadan, ‘and fasting three days of every month’: so after Ramadan it suffices to fast three days of every month throughout the year, and this equals fasting the whole year, since every good deed is worth ten like it; a month is worth ten months, and three days of every month equal the whole month.” - al-Durar al-Saniyyah (dorar.net)

The benefit of fasting is reaped through repetition; as al-Asqalani pointed out regarding the hadith “O young people! … should fast,” what is meant is fasting again and again. That is what bears the fruit and sharpens insight
