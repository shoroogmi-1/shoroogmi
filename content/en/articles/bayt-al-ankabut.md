Title: How Do You Build a House Stronger Than the Spider's House?
Excerpt: Surat al-Ankabut began with striving against the self (And whoever strives only strives for [the benefit of] himself. Indeed, Allah is free from need of the worlds (6) (Al-Ankabut)

Surat al-Ankabut began with striving against the self (And whoever strives only strives for [the benefit of] himself. Indeed, Allah is free from need of the worlds (6) (Al-Ankabut)

And it ended with striving (And those who strive for Us — We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good (69) (Al-Ankabut)

The surah has not ended yet! For after it came:

(Alif, Lam, Meem (1) The Byzantines have been defeated (2) In the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome (3) Within three to nine years. To Allah belongs the command before and after. And that day the believers will rejoice (4) In the victory of Allah. He gives victory to whom He wills, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful (5) [It is] the promise of Allah. Allah does not fail in His promise, but most of the people do not know (6) (Ar-Rum)

Striving against the self in obedience and in leaving sin: a road whose beginning is trials (Alif, Lam, Meem. Do the people think that they will be left to say, "We believe" and they will not be tried? (2) But We have certainly tried those before them, and Allah will surely make evident those who are truthful, and He will surely make evident the liars (3)(Al-Ankabut)

Do those on their way to their Lord think they will not be tried and tested!? No, rather the one who is truthful in his claim must be distinguished from the one who lies.

If there were a programmer's job and 2,000 applicants came for it, each claiming to be the best, wouldn't it be fair for the hiring manager to test their abilities and claims, by putting a computer in front of them and saying: You're a programmer? Let me see your work! And test him on the program.

And to Allah belongs the highest example: if everyone who said "I am a believer and righteous" were taken at their word, everyone would enter Paradise! There has to be a process of filtering and sorting, and intensive, successive tests, from a hard test to a harder one; all of this for the quality of the sorting and then the choosing, for the Most Merciful does not choose just anyone for His abode, but those who passed the tests with success and distinction.

And there are those whose selves grew too weak to strive, and who turned back on their heels

His weakness in striving is like the weakness of the spider's house, for her house is the frailest: the weakest, and I also see in the meaning lowliness, humiliation, and smallness; if you blew on it, all she built would be gone.

(The example of those who take allies other than Allah is like that of the spider who takes a home. And indeed, the weakest of homes is the home of the spider, if they only knew (41) (Al-Ankabut)

And part of the heart's weakness is that if good befalls it, it rejoices and says Allah loves me, and if a calamity befalls it, it abandons what it was upon

(And of the people are some who say, "We believe in Allah," but when one [of them] is harmed for [the cause of] Allah, they consider the trial of the people as [if it were] the punishment of Allah. But if victory comes from your Lord, they say, "Indeed, We were with you." Is not Allah most knowing of what is within the breasts of all creatures? (10) And Allah will surely make evident those who believe, and He will surely make evident the hypocrites (11) (Al-Ankabut)

And the strength of the believer's heart lies in his being one and the same in all conditions, devoted to the One, neither changing nor shifting

The states of time upon you are many and varied

Yet your state is one in every state (al-Mutanabbi)

Why do you think Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, placed the story of Noah and Abraham (peace be upon them) in Surat al-Ankabut? Because their building is lofty and firm. Before you is Noah (peace be upon him), who was patient for a thousand years minus fifty, an example to follow in strength of heart and long endurance:

(And We certainly sent Noah to his people, and he remained among them a thousand years minus fifty years, and the flood seized them while they were wrongdoers (14) But We saved him and the companions of the ship, and We made it a sign for the worlds (15) (Al-Ankabut)

And the self never weakens the way it weakens over the matter of provision! A person may be among the most devoted of people in worship and the most knowledgeable, yet the matter of provision keeps him up at night, and it is the reason he abandons all kinds of striving!

So when provision comes to him, he leaves the recommended deeds he used to do and perhaps falls short in the obligatory ones, and if he finds it slow in coming, he says: My Lord has humiliated me! And because provision is the most important standard by which Allah distinguishes the truthful from the liar, He makes it come late! So that there is a period of refining and testing, sufficient and complete, to distinguish the believer from the hypocrite

(And [We sent] Abraham, when he said to his people, "Worship Allah and fear Him. That is best for you, if you should know (16) You only worship, besides Allah, idols, and you produce a falsehood. Indeed, those you worship besides Allah do not possess for you [the power of] provision. So seek from Allah provision and worship Him and be grateful to Him. To Him you will be returned." (17) (Al-Ankabut)

Whoever does not understand this road will be greatly delayed and even more shaken.

Among them are those who despaired and lost hope because of delayed provision and the many trials, so they abandoned striving and said: This is nothing but legends of the former peoples.

(And the ones who disbelieve in the signs of Allah and the meeting with Him — those have despaired of My mercy, and they will have a painful punishment (23) (Al-Ankabut)

And among them are those given the resolve of Abraham (peace be upon him), so they were patient and strove until Allah saved them by His grace and generosity

(And the answer of his people was not but that they said, "Kill him or burn him," but Allah saved him from the fire. Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe (24) (Al-Ankabut)

And I picture this scene: Abraham (peace be upon him) came out of the fire and carried on calling to Allah the Exalted! As if nothing had happened. That is why this verse came after it:

(And [Abraham] said, "You have only taken, other than Allah, idols as [a bond of] affection among you in worldly life. Then on the Day of Resurrection you will deny one another and curse one another, and your refuge will be the Fire, and you will not have any helpers." (25) (Al-Ankabut)

And among the most important signs of striving: emigration (*hijrah*), by forsaking every evil you were settled in, whether beliefs, ideas, behaviors, relationships, or surroundings. And know that emigration is very costly, and without it the truthful cannot be distinguished from the liar! So whoever strives for Allah as He deserves must take up the worship of leaving, not obedience alone: “The emigrant is the one who forsakes evil deeds” “the one who forsakes what Allah has forbidden” and in one wording he said “the one who forsakes evil.”

Emigration and sacrifice are among the hardest of tests: that you leave what you love for the sake of a greater Beloved (And Lot believed him. [Abraham] said, "Indeed, I will emigrate to [the service of] my Lord. Indeed, He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise." (26) (Al-Ankabut)

(O My servants who have believed, indeed My earth is spacious, so worship only Me (56) (Al-Ankabut)

My earth is spacious: sins have substitutes among acts of obedience, gossip and idle talk have a replacement in beneficial knowledge, a bad friend is replaced by a righteous friend, goodness and mercy, and better and more ample than unlawful money is lawful, blessed money. You do not leave anything but that something more beautiful and more beneficial takes its place; (And whoever emigrates for the cause of Allah will find on the earth many locations and abundance) (An-Nisa) It is as if repentance and striving are a journey and a voyage to a more beautiful city, a roomier and more spacious place than the one you lived in.

And the reward for all that patience and that hardship and striving:

(And We gave to him Isaac and Jacob and placed in his descendants prophethood and scripture. And We gave him his reward in this world, and indeed, he is in the Hereafter among the righteous (27).(Al-Ankabut)

And in this verse He placed the greatest aid to striving, especially to forsaking sins: reciting the Quran, and prayer

(Recite, [O Muhammad], what has been revealed to you of the Book and establish prayer. Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater. And Allah knows that which you do (45) (Al-Ankabut)

Then He mentioned the merit of the one who memorizes the Quran: that his house is firmer in piety, and it is the strongest and sturdiest of houses:

(Rather, it [i.e., the Quran] is distinct verses [preserved] within the breasts of those who have been given knowledge. And none reject Our verses except the wrongdoers (49) (Al-Ankabut)

And the one who has nothing of the Quran inside him to recite and memorize is toyed with by whispers, and the slightest passing thought shakes his steadiness and certainty; desires lead him astray, doubts assassinate him, devils make things seem fair to him, and everything plays with his emotions. And the problem is that he does not know that the cause of his heart's sickness and his self's weakness is his distance from the Quran; whoever forsakes the Quran, evil does not forsake him:

“Indeed, the one who has nothing of the Quran inside him is like the ruined house”

But the one who has a daily portion of the Quran and memorizes some of it, you find his house among the strongest of houses and the firmest in standing; his richness is in his heart, and his sufficiency endures through his Lord

(And is it not sufficient for them that We revealed to you the Book which is recited to them? Indeed in that is a mercy and reminder for a people who believe (51) (Al-Ankabut)

"Whoever's beginning shines, his end shines"

Whoever's beginning shines with striving and repentance, his end shines by his becoming one of the doers of good

And it ended with striving (And those who strive for Us — We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good (69) (Al-Ankabut)

Imam al-Ghazali (may Allah have mercy on him) said: Upon the servant is to take up the means of striving (And those who strive for Us) and upon Allah is guidance (We will surely guide them to Our ways)

And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good: those who strove; He named their striving: doing good (*iḥsān*)!

And Allah is with the doer of good by His companionship, protection, and support, and that is why after Surat al-Ankabut came: Ar-Rum

(Alif, Lam, Meem (1) The Byzantines have been defeated (2) In the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome (3) Within three to nine years. To Allah belongs the command before and after. And that day the believers will rejoice (4) In the victory of Allah. He gives victory to whom He wills, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful (5) [It is] the promise of Allah. Allah does not fail in His promise, but most of the people do not know (6) (Ar-Rum)

By Allah, whoever strove against himself has won!

He has won against the enemies of the self, against whims, and against the devils among men and jinn!

This feeling of great victory is sweeter than all the certificates he has earned and the achievements he has reaped, something indescribable! He feels he is a king walking the earth, and a bright star in the sky!

The one who has won against himself is worthy of winning against the outside enemy, however great its strength:

Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him) said:

"You do not defeat your enemy by numbers, nor by equipment, but you defeat them by this religion; so if you and your enemy become equal in sins, victory will be theirs"

So build a strong house whose beginning is striving and whose end is striving, then doing good, then victory.
