Title: How Do You Build a House Stronger Than the Spider's?
Excerpt: Surat al-Ankabut opens with the struggle against the self (And whoever strives only strives for [the benefit of] himself. Indeed, Allah is free from need of the worlds (6) (Al-Ankabut)

Surat al-Ankabut opens with the struggle against the self (And whoever strives only strives for [the benefit of] himself. Indeed, Allah is free from need of the worlds (6) (Al-Ankabut)

And it closes with striving (And those who strive for Us — We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good (69) (Al-Ankabut)

Yet the surah has not truly ended! For right after it comes:

(Alif, Lam, Meem (1) The Byzantines have been defeated (2) In the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome (3) Within three to nine years. To Allah belongs the command before and after. And that day the believers will rejoice (4) In the victory of Allah. He gives victory to whom He wills, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful (5) [It is] the promise of Allah. Allah does not fail in His promise, but most of the people do not know (6) (Ar-Rum)

Striving against the self — to obey, and to leave sin — is a road that begins with trials (Alif, Lam, Meem. Do the people think that they will be left to say, "We believe" and they will not be tried? (2) But We have certainly tried those before them, and Allah will surely make evident those who are truthful, and He will surely make evident the liars (3)(Al-Ankabut)

Do those journeying to their Lord imagine they will not be tried and tested!? Not at all: the one who is true to his claim must be told apart from the one who lies.

Picture a job opening for a programmer, and 2,000 applicants, each claiming to be the best. Wouldn't it be only fair for the hiring manager to put their skills and their claims to the test — to sit each one in front of a computer and say: You're a programmer? Show me your work! — and test him on the program?

And to Allah belongs the highest attribute: if everyone who said "I am a believer, I am righteous" were taken at their word, everyone would enter Paradise! There must be a process of sifting and sorting, intensive tests one after another, each harder than the last — all for the sake of a true sorting, and then the choosing; for the Most Merciful does not choose just anyone for His abode, only those who pass the tests with success and distinction.

And there are those whose souls grew too weak for the struggle, and who turned back on their heels

Such weakness in striving is like the weakness of the spider's house, for hers is the flimsiest house of all — *awhan*: the weakest — and I see in the word, too, a sense of lowliness, disgrace and smallness: blow on it, and all she has built is gone.

(The example of those who take allies other than Allah is like that of the spider who takes a home. And indeed, the weakest of homes is the home of the spider, if they only knew (41) (Al-Ankabut)

And it is a sign of a weak heart that when good comes its way, it rejoices and says, Allah loves me! — and when calamity strikes, it abandons the path it was on

(And of the people are some who say, "We believe in Allah," but when one [of them] is harmed for [the cause of] Allah, they consider the trial of the people as [if it were] the punishment of Allah. But if victory comes from your Lord, they say, "Indeed, We were with you." Is not Allah most knowing of what is within the breasts of all creatures? (10) And Allah will surely make evident those who believe, and He will surely make evident the hypocrites (11) (Al-Ankabut)

The strength of the believer's heart lies in his being one and the same in every circumstance — devoted to the One, never changing, never turning

The turns of time upon you are many and varied,

yet you remain one and the same in every state (al-Mutanabbi)

Why do you think Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, placed the stories of Noah and Abraham, peace be upon them, in Surat al-Ankabut? Because what they built was lofty and firm. Before you stands Noah, peace be upon him, who endured for a thousand years minus fifty — a model to follow in strength of heart and long patience:

(And We certainly sent Noah to his people, and he remained among them a thousand years minus fifty years, and the flood seized them while they were wrongdoers (14) But We saved him and the companions of the ship, and We made it a sign for the worlds (15) (Al-Ankabut)

And nothing weakens the soul the way the matter of provision does! A person may be among the most devout and most learned of people, yet worry over provision keeps him awake at night, and it is what drives him to abandon every kind of striving!

When provision comes to him, he drops the voluntary deeds he used to do and may even fall short in the obligatory ones; and when it is slow in coming, he says: My Lord has humiliated me! And because provision is the most important measure by which Allah tells the truthful from the liar, He lets it come late! — so that there is a time of refining and testing, full and sufficient, to tell the believer from the hypocrite

(And [We sent] Abraham, when he said to his people, "Worship Allah and fear Him. That is best for you, if you should know (16) You only worship, besides Allah, idols, and you produce a falsehood. Indeed, those you worship besides Allah do not possess for you [the power of] provision. So seek from Allah provision and worship Him and be grateful to Him. To Him you will be returned." (17) (Al-Ankabut)

Whoever does not understand this road will be held back long, and shaken even more.

Some despaired and lost all hope when provision was late and trials piled up; they abandoned the struggle and said: This is nothing but legends of the former peoples.

(And the ones who disbelieve in the signs of Allah and the meeting with Him — those have despaired of My mercy, and they will have a painful punishment (23) (Al-Ankabut)

And some were given the resolve of Abraham, peace be upon him: they endured and strove until Allah delivered them by His grace and generosity

(And the answer of Abraham's people was not but that they said, "Kill him or burn him," but Allah saved him from the fire. Indeed in that are signs for a people who believe (24) (Al-Ankabut)

And I picture the scene: Abraham, peace be upon him, walks out of the fire and carries on calling people to Allah the Exalted! As if nothing had happened. That is why this verse comes right after it:

(And [Abraham] said, "You have only taken, other than Allah, idols as [a bond of] affection among you in worldly life. Then on the Day of Resurrection you will deny one another and curse one another, and your refuge will be the Fire, and you will not have any helpers." (25) (Al-Ankabut)

Among the most important signs of striving is *hijrah*, emigration: forsaking every evil you had settled into, whether beliefs, ideas, habits, relationships or surroundings. Know that emigration is very costly, yet without it the truthful cannot be told from the liar! So whoever strives for Allah with the striving due to Him must take up the worship of leaving, not obedience alone: “The emigrant is the one who forsakes evil deeds,” “the one who gives up (abandons) all what Allah has forbidden,” and in one wording he said, “the one who forsakes evil.”

Emigration and sacrifice are among the hardest of tests: leaving what you love for the sake of a greater Beloved (And Lot believed him. [Abraham] said, "Indeed, I will emigrate to [the service of] my Lord. Indeed, He is the Exalted in Might, the Wise." (26) (Al-Ankabut)

(O My servants who have believed, indeed My earth is spacious, so worship only Me (56) (Al-Ankabut)

My earth is spacious: every sin has its replacement among acts of obedience; gossip and idle talk can be exchanged for beneficial knowledge; a bad friend makes way for a righteous one, a blessing and a mercy; and better and more ample than unlawful wealth is lawful, blessed wealth. You give nothing up but that something more beautiful and more beneficial takes its place; (And whoever emigrates for the cause of Allah will find on the earth many [alternative] locations and abundance) (An-Nisa) It is as if repentance and striving were a journey, a voyage to a lovelier city, a roomier and more spacious place than the one you used to live in.

And the reward for all that patience, all that hardship and striving:

(And We gave to him Isaac and Jacob and placed in his descendants prophethood and scripture. And We gave him his reward in this world, and indeed, he is in the Hereafter among the righteous (27).(Al-Ankabut)

And in this verse He set the greatest help in every struggle, above all the struggle to forsake sin: reciting the Quran, and prayer

(Recite, [O Muhammad], what has been revealed to you of the Book and establish prayer. Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater. And Allah knows that which you do (45) (Al-Ankabut)

Then He speaks of the merit of the one who carries the Quran in memory: his house is more firmly rooted in God-consciousness, the strongest and sturdiest of all houses:

(Rather, the Qur'an is distinct verses [preserved] within the breasts of those who have been given knowledge. And none reject Our verses except the wrongdoers (49) (Al-Ankabut)

But the one who has nothing of the Quran inside him to recite and hold in memory is a plaything for whispers; the faintest passing thought shakes his steadiness and certainty. Desires lead him astray, doubts strike him down, devils dress things up for him, and everything plays on his emotions. The trouble is, he does not realize that the cause of his heart's sickness and his soul's weakness is his distance from the Quran: whoever forsakes the Quran is never forsaken by evil:

“Indeed, the one who does not have the Qur'an inside him (his heart), is like the ruined house”

But the one who keeps a daily portion of the Quran and holds some of it in memory — you will find his house among the strongest of houses and the firmest in standing; his richness is in his heart, and his sufficiency endures through his Lord

(And is it not sufficient for them that We revealed to you the Book which is recited to them? Indeed in that is a mercy and reminder for a people who believe (51) (Al-Ankabut)

"Whoever has a radiant beginning will have a radiant end"

Whoever's beginning is radiant with striving and repentance will have a radiant end: he will become one of the doers of good

And it closes with striving (And those who strive for Us — We will surely guide them to Our ways. And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good (69) (Al-Ankabut)

Imam al-Ghazali, may Allah have mercy on him, said: It is for the servant to take up the means of striving (And those who strive for Us), and for Allah to grant guidance (We will surely guide them to Our ways)

And indeed, Allah is with the doers of good: those who strove — He called their striving *iḥsān*, doing good!

And Allah is with the one who does good — at his side, guarding him, granting him victory; and that is why Surat al-Ankabut is followed by Ar-Rum

(Alif, Lam, Meem (1) The Byzantines have been defeated (2) In the nearest land. But they, after their defeat, will overcome (3) Within three to nine years. To Allah belongs the command before and after. And that day the believers will rejoice (4) In the victory of Allah. He gives victory to whom He wills, and He is the Exalted in Might, the Merciful (5) [It is] the promise of Allah. Allah does not fail in His promise, but most of the people do not know (6) (Ar-Rum)

By Allah, whoever has striven against himself has triumphed!

He has triumphed over the enemies of the soul, over his own whims, and over the devils among men and jinn!

This feeling of a great victory is sweeter than every certificate he has earned and every achievement he has reaped — something beyond words! He feels like a king walking upon the earth, a bright star shining in the sky!

Whoever has triumphed over himself is worthy of triumphing over the enemy outside, however great its strength:

Umar ibn al-Khattab, may Allah be pleased with him, said:

"You do not defeat your enemy by numbers or by equipment; you defeat them by this religion. If you and your enemy become equal in sins, victory will be theirs"

So build a strong house: one that begins with striving and ends with striving, then doing good, then victory.
