Title: Inner Strength Is a Gift from Allah
Excerpt: Every kind of inner strength — patience in all its forms, steadfastness amid trials, courage when you are few, joy and gladness when calamities descend! — all of it is a gift from Allah the Exalted, His support for His servant, and none of it springs from the servant himself

Every kind of inner strength — patience in all its forms, steadfastness amid trials, courage when you are few, joy and gladness when calamities descend! — all of it is a gift from Allah the Exalted, His support for His servant, and none of it springs from the servant himself

Have you not heard His words: (It is He who sent down tranquillity into the hearts of the believers)? And His words: (And be patient, [O Muhammad], and your patience is not but through Allah)

This support descends upon the believer's soul when it is filled with the spirit of the Quran. "*Ta'yīd* (support) is strengthening, and enabling for the work of the soul. It is derived from *al-ayd*, which is strength; the Exalted said: ﴿and remember Our servant, David, the possessor of strength﴾ [Sad: 17]. And *al-ayd* is derived from *al-yad* (the hand), since the hand is the instrument of power; the better view is that it is derived from *al-yad*, meaning He made him one possessed of a hand, that is, of strength. What is meant here is an inner strength: the strength of the message, and the strength to bear patiently the harm of his people." — Ibn Ashur

Patience in carrying the message of the Quran — acting on it, drawing nourishment from it, pondering it and understanding it — comes from Allah's support and His granting of success, not from the servant himself. And patience in carrying the message is what yields patience of the other kinds: patience through calamity, and patience in holding back from sin. Both are the fruit of patience in obedience, and the greatest and nearest of all acts of obedience is the Quran.
