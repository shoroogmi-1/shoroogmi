Title: After the Verses of Victory, Allah Speaks of Wealth!
Excerpt: In Al-Baqarah: (Or do you think that you will enter Paradise…. until [even their] messenger and those who believed with him said, "When is the help of Allah?" Unquestionably, the help of Allah is near) and then comes (They ask you, [O Muhammad], what they should spend..)

In Al-Baqarah: (Or do you think that you will enter Paradise…. until [even their] messenger and those who believed with him said, "When is the help of Allah?" Unquestionably, the help of Allah is near) and then comes (They ask you, [O Muhammad], what they should spend..)

When He willed to affirm that victory would come without fail — while minds had come to doubt it, so long had it tarried — He set down, to seal that certainty and banish every doubt, the rulings on spending the spoils!

As if to say: you will be granted victory, without fail and beyond doubt; but that is not what matters. What matters more is this: what will you do, after victory, with the wealth and provision that come your way? Be mindful of Allah in it, and spend it as He has ordained.

In Al Imran:

He mentions the Battle of Badr (And already had Allah given you victory at [the battle of] Badr while you were few in number. Then fear Allah; perhaps you will be grateful), then He mentions the prohibition of consuming usury (O you who have believed, do not consume usury, doubled and multiplied..)

Fear Allah regarding the victory He gave you, and thank Him by leaving off sin: do not consume usury and slide into arrogance and insolence. Indeed, victory is one of the moments for seeking forgiveness, for whoever is granted a conquest. Allah commanded the Children of Israel, when they entered (the city: Jerusalem), to say *ḥiṭṭah*, meaning "O Allah, take our sins away from us"; but they went in saying *ḥinṭah* (wheat). They did not give thanks; they disobeyed (And [recall] when We said, "Enter this city… and say, 'Relieve us of our burdens' [*ḥiṭṭah*]")

And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ entered Makkah in conquest with his head bowed, reciting the verses of his Lord and seeking forgiveness (When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest, and you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes, then exalt [Him] with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance)

Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him; I seek Allah's forgiveness and turn to Him in repentance.
