Title: Be Kind to Your Children and Perfect Their Manners
Excerpt: From the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who said: “Be kind to your children, and perfect their manners.”

From the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who said: “Be kind to your children, and perfect their manners.”

His words “Be kind to your children”: for kindness toward them only deepens their love for their parents. But because kindness may lead to bad manners, he pointed, with his words “and perfect their manners,” to the fact that kindness ought not to go that far.
