Among the truths of spiritual knowing: whatever fancies, schemes, and wishes you turn your mind away from come walking toward you in reality, easy and unforced —if Allah wills—.

But if you arrange every last detail and forget the One who arranges all things, in whose hand are the decrees, to whom belong the reins of all affairs, and in whose hand are the keys; if you have reckoned with everything and counted every cost, then how is He to provide for you from where you do not expect!?

Yes, we take the means; to neglect them is helplessness and sloth. But do not let your soul cling to these wishes too fiercely, for such clinging is a kind of craving and greed, and that does not become one who truly knows Allah. Have you not heard the words of the Exalted: (and We will surely turn you to a qiblah with which you will be pleased)?

He said *tarḍāhā*, “with which you will be pleased,” and not *tahwāhā*, “which you crave”; for his soul ﷺ was too perfect and too noble to hang upon a wish or a dream.

And Moses (peace be upon him), when he counted out all that his staff could do: (He said, “It is my staff; I lean upon it, and I bring down leaves for my sheep and I have therein other uses.”)

([Allah] said, “Throw it down, O Moses.”) He meant to teach him that the soul must not cling to any means, so He made the staff fearsome to him: (So he threw it down, and thereupon it was a snake, moving swiftly).

([Allah] said, “Seize it and fear not; We will return it to its former condition.”)

Now! Take it up by My leave and My will, not by your leave and your choosing!

And once he had let it go and turned from it, Allah placed in it the miracle and the victory: it swallowed the sorcerers’ ropes, it split the sea, and through it they were granted victory even after his death.

So whatever we let go of most completely, He has replaced with something better, beyond anything we could imagine.

And when Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, nurtures those who truly know Him, He raises them toward the most perfect of souls, through the purest letting go, and then being set free.
