(And recite to them, [O Muhammad], the news of him to whom we gave [knowledge of] Our signs, but he detached himself from them; so Satan pursued him, and he became of the deviators) A parable Allah has struck for the one who was given the Quran and its clear proofs, yet did not act upon it. What made him swerve from guidance: following his desire in place of the Book, and preferring this world to the Hereafter.

But he detached himself from them: he cast off faith altogether, never to return.

So Satan pursued him: this is as grave as it gets. The sinner follows Satan; but this man was a believing scholar who, after all his knowledge and faith, became a disbeliever, so Satan came after him and followed at his heels, and he became Satan's leader! And Satan his follower! See the eloquence of the parable; and all for the enormity of what he had done

“The more abundant Allah the Exalted's blessings upon a person, the farther and greater his distance from Allah, should he turn away from Him; this is made plain by the words of Allah the Exalted: And recite to them the news of him to whom We gave Our signs …” al-Tafsir al-Muharrar.

(And if We had willed, we could have elevated him thereby, but he adhered [instead] to the earth and followed his own desire. So his example is like that of the dog: if you chase him, he pants, or if you leave him, he [still] pants. That is the example of the people who denied Our signs. So relate the stories that perhaps they will give thought)

And if We had willed, we could have elevated him thereby, in this world and the Hereafter; but he sank down to (the earth: this world) and settled there (Ibn Kathir)

So whether you rebuke him or leave him be, he is like the dog: pick up a stone and throw it at him, or leave him alone, and still he pants. And this man, whether you admonish him or not, never stops, never turns back from the misguidance he is in; he pants after this world, selling his soul to buy it.

A lesson: the servant must do all he can to remain steadfast, by putting the Hereafter before this world, and choosing to follow the Quran over following his desire, so that Allah the Exalted may make him firm
