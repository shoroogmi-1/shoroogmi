Title: Guidance and Good Tidings
Excerpt: Again and again the Quran says (guidance and good tidings); so what is the secret behind this order?

Again and again the Quran says (guidance and good tidings); so what is the secret behind this order?

They achieved guidance first, and so they earned, second, the news of good tidings (al-Tafsir al-Muharrar)

I say: one proof of this is the word of Allah the Exalted (Unquestionably, [for] the allies of Allah there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve. Those who believed and were fearing Allah. For them are good tidings in the worldly life and in the Hereafter. No change is there in the words of Allah. That is what is the great attainment)

They took up the means of guidance, so they were granted Allah's friendship, and for it they won the prize of good tidings.
