Title: My Lord, Make Me Forget What I Have Spent, and Help Me with What I Have Left

When Qaylah (may Allah be pleased with her) lost her son and wept before the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and complained to him of the responsibilities he had left upon her:

“So I wept, then said: By Allah, O Messenger of Allah, I bore him resolute, and he fought alongside you on the day of al-Rabadhah, then he went to bring me provisions from Khaybar, and its fever struck him, and he left the women to me”

So he advised her to supplicate, saying: “My Lord, make me forget what I have spent, and help me with what I have left” (a hasan hadith)

In it is that a person should supplicate that Allah make him forget the past and what he lost in it, and help him with what remains of his life. And in it is that the best way to deal with the past that is gone is forgetting, and if he cannot forget, let him ask Allah to make him forget, and turn to the present and look after it, and ask his Lord for help with the burdens and affairs he is undertaking, and not think that help comes from anyone other than Allah.

“By the One in whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, were you not a poor woman, we would have dragged you on your face today, or you would have been dragged on your face (Abdullah was unsure). Is one of you unable to keep good company with his little companion in this world, and then, when one more entitled to him than he comes between them, say: ‘Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return’”

For Allah is more entitled than you to the one you lost.
