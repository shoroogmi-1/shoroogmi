(And whatever good you do – Allah knows it): why did He leave out the word (or evil?)

"When I know good from you, I mention it and make it known, and when I know evil from you, I cover it and conceal it; so that you may know that if My mercy toward you in this world is like this, then how will it be in the Hereafter."?

"The second of them: among the exegetes is one who said in explaining His saying: ﴿Indeed, the Hour is coming – I almost conceal it﴾ [Ta-Ha: 15] its meaning is: if it were possible for Me to conceal it from Myself, I would do so.

So it is with this verse; it is as if the servant were told: whatever good you do, I know it; as for the evil you do, if it were possible for Me to conceal it from Myself, I would do that." - al-Razi

O Allah, forgive us what they do not know, and make us better than what they think. I seek forgiveness from Allah the Almighty and repent to Him.
