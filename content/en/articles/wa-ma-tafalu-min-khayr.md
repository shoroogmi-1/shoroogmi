(And whatever good you do – Allah knows it): why did He hold back the word (or evil?)

"When I know good of you, I mention it and make it known; and when I know evil of you, I veil it and hide it — so that you may know: if this is how My mercy treats you in this world, then what will it be in the Hereafter?"

"The second: some of the exegetes said, explaining His words ﴿Indeed, the Hour is coming – I almost conceal it﴾ [Ta-Ha: 15], that it means: had it been possible for Me to conceal it even from Myself, I would have done so.

So too with this verse. It is as though the servant were told: whatever good you do, I know it; but the evil you do — had it been possible for Me to conceal it even from Myself, I would have done so." - al-Razi

O Allah, forgive us what they do not know of us, and make us better than they think us to be. I seek the forgiveness of Allah, the Almighty, and I turn to Him in repentance.
