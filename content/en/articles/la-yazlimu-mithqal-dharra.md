Title: (Indeed, Allah does not do injustice, [even] as much as an atom's weight; while if there is a good deed, He multiplies it and gives from Himself a great reward)
Excerpt: “Allah does not wrong a believer of a good deed: he is given provision for it in this world and is rewarded for it in the Hereafter. As for the disbeliever, he is fed for it in this world, and when the Day of Resurrection comes, he will have no good deed left.” Sahih Muslim
