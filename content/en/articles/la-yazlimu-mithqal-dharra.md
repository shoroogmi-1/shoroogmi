Title: (Indeed, Allah does not do injustice, [even] as much as an atom's weight; while if there is a good deed, He multiplies it and gives from Himself a great reward)
Excerpt: “Allah does not wrong a believer in a single good deed: he is rewarded for it with provision in this world and recompensed for it in the Hereafter. As for the disbeliever, he is fed for it in this world, so that when the Day of Resurrection comes, he has no good deed at all.” Sahih Muslim
