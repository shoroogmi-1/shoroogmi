Title: (For them who have done good is the best [reward])
Excerpt: (And speak to people good [words])

(And speak to people good [words])

You will not die until you have received your full portion of provision from the praise of believers and the hearing of kind words.

There is something like a balance, an account, of kind words written for you to hear in this life, and you will not die until you have drawn it down to the last word!
