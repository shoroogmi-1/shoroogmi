Title: (For them who have done good is the best [reward])
Excerpt: (And speak to people good [words])

(And speak to people good [words])

You will not die until you have received your full provision of the praise of believers and of hearing a good word.

There is something like a balance, an account, of good words decreed for you to hear in this life, and you will not die until you have used up this account!
