Title: (Look at your food and your drink; it has not changed with time)
Excerpt: It is one of Allah's gentle kindnesses to His servant: when He lets his life die — in wealth, in health, in loneliness, and in other ways — and then brings it back to life anew as healing, as provision, as companionship, the servant feels that nothing was taken from him at all. And truly, nothing was, for Allah's mending is greater and His generosity more abundant.
