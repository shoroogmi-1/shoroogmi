Among the names of the Qur'an that Fakhr al-Din al-Razi lists is *al-Qaṣaṣ*, “the Narratives” (We relate to you, [O Muhammad], the best of stories), so named because the Qur'an's traces and verses must be followed, tracked step by step the way skilled trackers read a trail, until one becomes adept at it (And she said to his sister, "Follow him")

And when he came to explain the verses of legal retribution (*al-qiṣāṣ*), he, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

"The fourth view: Abu al-Jawza' read ﴿And there is for you in legal retribution (*al-qiṣāṣ*) [saving of] life﴾ as meaning: in what He has narrated (*qaṣṣa*) to you of the rulings on killing and retribution. And it has been said that *al-qiṣāṣ* here is the Qur'an, meaning: in the Qur'an there is life for you, life for the hearts, as in His words ﴿an inspiration (*rūḥ*) of Our command﴾ [Ash-Shura: 52] and ﴿and those who lived [in faith] would live upon evidence﴾ [Al-Anfal: 42]. And Allah knows best."
