Title: (Whoever is an enemy to Allah and His angels and His messengers and Gabriel and Michael – then indeed, Allah is an enemy to the disbelievers)
Excerpt: Allah the Exalted names Gabriel before Michael (peace be upon them both) because the one who carries the Quran stands high, even when he is an angel! The bearer of the Quran is named before all others, for the sake of the lofty thing that has settled in his breast

Allah the Exalted names Gabriel before Michael (peace be upon them both) because the one who carries the Quran stands high, even when he is an angel! The bearer of the Quran is named before all others, for the sake of the lofty thing that has settled in his breast

"Gabriel (peace be upon him) descends with the Quran, with revelation and knowledge, and these are what keep souls alive; Michael descends with fertile harvests and rain, and these are what keep bodies alive. And since knowledge is nobler than food, Gabriel must be the more excellent of the two." — al-Razi.
