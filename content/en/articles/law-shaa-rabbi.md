Title: Had My Lord Willed, I Would Have Been Qays ibn Khalid
Excerpt: Had my Lord willed, I would have been Qays ibn Khalid

Had my Lord willed, I would have been Qays ibn Khalid

and had my Lord willed, I would have been Amr ibn Marthad

— Tarafa ibn al-Abd

"These are two lords among the lords of the Arabs, renowned for abundant wealth and noble children, honorable lineage and great standing. He says: had Allah willed, He would have brought me to their rank and worth."( al-Zawzani)

It reminded me of the saying of Allah the Exalted consoling His Prophet ﷺ, when the polytheists said: why does he have no treasure, nor a garden from which he eats?

(Blessed is He who, if He willed, could make for you better than that — gardens beneath which rivers flow — and could make for you palaces (10) (Al-Furqan)
