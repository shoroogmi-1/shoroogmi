Title: Had My Lord Willed, I Would Have Been Qays ibn Khalid
Excerpt: Had my Lord willed, I would have been Qays ibn Khalid;

Had my Lord willed, I would have been Qays ibn Khalid;

had my Lord willed, I would have been Amr ibn Marthad.

— Tarafa ibn al-Abd

"These are two of the chiefs of the Arabs, famed for their abundant wealth and noble sons, their high lineage and great standing. He is saying: had Allah willed, He would have raised me to their rank and worth." (al-Zawzani)

It brought to mind the words of Allah the Exalted consoling His Prophet ﷺ when the polytheists asked: why has he no treasure, no garden to eat from?

(Blessed is He who, if He willed, could have made for you [something] better than that — gardens beneath which rivers flow — and could make for you palaces (10) (Al-Furqan)
