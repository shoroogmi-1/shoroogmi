The research site PubMed Central (PMC), part of the U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH), published a study that organizes reliable and strong evidence on the effect of the Noble Quran on mental and physical health, titled:

The Impact of Listening to, Reciting, or Memorizing the Quran on Physical and Mental Health of Muslims: Evidence From Systematic Review

In it, the researchers gathered 230 studies published on major, highly ranked scientific sites, such as PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and Google Scholar. They did not choose just any study; rather, they selected those studies after evaluating their methodology and analyzing their data to make sure they were not results influenced by chance or bias, then they organized their evidence and findings, and the results were dazzling:

- Listening to Surah Ar-Rahman had a great effect on managing depression in pregnant women, and reduced their anxiety about childbirth.

- Listening to Surah Ya-Sin has a great psychological effect on the sense of tranquility and reassurance.

- Listening to the Noble Quran in general lowered the rates of depression and anxiety symptoms in kidney patients and heart patients.

- Listening to the Quran improves sleep quality

- Amazingly, when intensive care patients heard the Noble Quran, their vital sign readings decreased and their consciousness increased!

- Listening to the Quran improves physiological signs (regulating heartbeat and breathing..)

- Listening to the Quran improves quality of life and average lifespan.

- The Quran gives the mind bright and optimistic beliefs in its view of life and the events and calamities that befall it, which makes it more cohesive and strong.

- The more Quran is memorized (surahs, juz', methods of review, and so on), the higher the IQ.

- Memorizing the Quran affects brain atrophy, as the volume of gray and white matter and total brain volume were larger in those who had memorized the whole Quran than in those who had not memorized it or had memorized part of it; the explanation for this is that the one who memorizes uses the brain tissue and activates it continuously, which helps preserve its tissue.
