Title: The Righteous Friend
Excerpt: The righteous friend wants you for your Lord, not for himself,

The righteous friend wants you for your Lord, not for himself,

he wants no favors and asks you for no money; his whole relationship with you revolves around obedience: if you obey, he draws you near, and if you disobey, he rebukes you, and if you do not stop, he keeps away from you. So know that he is saving you for the Hereafter and has no need of your worldly life.

He lifts your resolve toward lofty things and accompanies you to them when determination slackens and aspirations weaken, and he prays for you and his prayers for you are answered.

And in the Hereafter he will be with you on pulpits of light, for which the prophets and martyrs will envy you.

Not every prophet has a righteous friend; some of them will be resurrected alone on the Day of Resurrection. And the friend intercedes for the believers and for those he knew in this world, after Allah gives permission and is pleased with him,

and he is the regret of the disbelievers, who say in the Fire, with a moan that rends the heart, (So now we have no intercessors and not a devoted friend. Then if we only had a return, we could be of the believers): if only I were sent back to life and became a believer honored by Allah so that I could intercede, or at the very least a righteous friend who knows me and whom I know would intercede for me!

This provision comes only from your Lord, so if you have been deprived of it, ask Him and call on Him, and do not give up or grow weary: say, O Allah, grant me and make easy for me good, righteous companions with whom I will be on pulpits of light on the Day of Resurrection.

O people, listen and understand, and know that Allah has servants who are neither prophets nor martyrs, whom the prophets and martyrs envy for their positions and their nearness to Allah. A man from among the Bedouins, from the far edge of the people, knelt down and gestured with his hand toward the Prophet ﷺ and said: O Messenger of Allah: people from among the people who are neither prophets nor martyrs, whom the prophets and martyrs envy for their gatherings and their nearness [to Allah]? Describe them to us, depict them for us - meaning, describe them to us. The face of the Prophet was gladdened by the Bedouin's question, and he said: They are people from among the unknown folk of humanity and strangers from various tribes, between whom there are no close ties of kinship; they loved one another for the sake of Allah and were sincere toward one another. On the Day of Resurrection Allah will set up for them pulpits of light, and they will sit upon them, and He will make their faces light and their garments light. The people will be terrified on the Day of Resurrection, but they will not be terrified, and they are the allies of Allah, upon whom there will be no fear, nor will they grieve. Hadith hasan
