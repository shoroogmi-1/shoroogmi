Title: (And during the night prostrate to Him and exalt Him a long [part of the] night)
Excerpt: It is as though He were saying: spend the greater part of the night with Me, glorifying Me: in prayer and prostration, in supplication and seeking forgiveness, in speaking to Me and whispering to Me in private. "O company of disciples, speak to Allah more than you speak to people." This time is His alone: not yours, and not the world's.

It is as though He were saying: spend the greater part of the night with Me, glorifying Me: in prayer and prostration, in supplication and seeking forgiveness, in speaking to Me and whispering to Me in private. "O company of disciples, speak to Allah more than you speak to people." This time is His alone: not yours, and not the world's.

The day belongs to you and to people; yet in all you do, let your intention be for Him, with sincerity and with patience.
