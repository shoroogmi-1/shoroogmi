Title: What Is the Bright Side of Artificial Intelligence?
Excerpt: The lesson was so hard that even its teacher told us it was not easy to learn.

The lesson was so hard that even its teacher told us it was not easy to learn.

Artificial intelligence taught it to me in 10 minutes, and I understood it with ease, through example after example.

I doubted what AI could really do, and feared it was only flattening my mind! So I went to a recorded academic lecture on the subject, given by (an actual human being)!

The very same examples the AI had given me — he used them! And when I say the same, I mean exactly the same!

And I realized I had spent 40 minutes of my life on a human lecture to get what AI gave me in 10.

AI is just like a teacher: a good teacher explains things to you with perfect clarity, is sincere with you, and makes the material simple, because he wants you to surpass him; and surely you have met the bad teacher too, the one who tangles the material, stretches the explanation far beyond what it needs, and leaves it all in fog.

So take AI as an expert coach and teacher in whatever fields you want to grow in! ☀️
