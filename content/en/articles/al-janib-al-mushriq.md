Title: What Is the Bright Side of Artificial Intelligence?
Excerpt: The lesson was very hard, so much so that its own teacher told us it was not easy to learn.

The lesson was very hard, so much so that its own teacher told us it was not easy to learn.

Artificial intelligence taught it to me in 10 minutes, and I understood it easily, with plenty of illustrative examples.

I doubted AI's ability and feared it was just flattening my mind! So I went to a recorded academic lecture on the subject given by (one of the children of Adam)!

The very same illustrative examples the AI gave, he used! When I say the same, I mean exactly the same!

So I realized I had wasted 40 minutes of my life on a human lecture versus 10 minutes with AI.

AI is just like a teacher: a good teacher explains things to you with great clarity, is sincere with you, and simplifies the material, because he wants you to be better than him; and you have certainly come across the bad teacher who complicates the material, drags out the explanation more than necessary, and makes it obscure.

So consider AI an expert coach and teacher in the fields you want to develop yourself in! ☀️
