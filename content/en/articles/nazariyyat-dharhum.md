Title: The "Leave Them" Theory
Excerpt: Leave them to do what they do: blocking, rejecting, scheming, and driving away are by the will of Allah! None can remove that but Him!
Quote: Leave them to rejoice in the little; ahead of them is much to keep them busy..

Leave them to do what they do: blocking, rejecting, scheming, and driving away are by the will of Allah! None can remove that but Him!

Because your attempt to correct them, change them, convince them, and win their affection falls under "the desire to control," and you cannot control anything but yourself.

That is why there is no use in concerning yourself with them!

{ And thus We have made for every prophet an enemy — devils from mankind and jinn, inspiring to one another decorative speech in delusion. But if your Lord had willed, they would not have done it, so leave them and that which they invent } [Surat al-An'am: 112]

And if your Lord had willed, they would have been on your side and dear to you! But He willed, for a wisdom, that they be your enemies. So can you change something Allah has written for you? No.

So the best way to deal with it: leave them to lie! Let them scheme! And let them mock you.

Leave them! Because letting go is not leaving life in chaos; rather, it is allowing yourself to organize your life instead of being preoccupied with that terrible, overwhelming chaos the agitated leave behind, which you can never, however hard you try, organize, understand, or put back in order!

"Letting go" does not mean merely letting things run as they are; it is a conscious choice to free the self from this primal urge to control" (Mel Robbins, p. 55)

So letting go does not mean you are weak. That comes from the illusion of feeling in control {You are not over them a controller }

[Surat al-Ghashiyah: 22]

Because feeling weak and helpless is a negative feeling about something you cannot fix or deal with, but what it means is that you leave others as they are! (Mel Robbins)

Leave them! They are the ones who chose loss, and they are the ones who chose misguidance.

So leave them; let the hopes they are in distract them, and let their numbered terms make what they are in seem fair to them, until they see it as everything and the greatest thing!

Leave them! For you are not responsible for them or held to account for them.

Leave them, for they will be repaid for their evil deeds in this world before the Hereafter, but it is a matter of time, so be patient and let them be. { Let them eat and enjoy themselves and be diverted by [false] hope, for they are going to know } [Surat al-Hijr: 3]

Leave them to rejoice in the little; ahead of them is much to keep them busy..

What they are in is nothing but being led on step by step, but their sensors are switched off, so they see it as blessing, opening, provision, and victory. Leave them in their arrogance, increasing to the point of tyranny, so they rebel and become wretched.

(But the people divided their religion among them into sects — each faction, in what it has, rejoicing (53) So leave them in their confusion for a time (54) Do they think that what We extend to them of wealth and children (55) Is [because] We hasten for them good things? Rather, they do not perceive (56). (Al-Mu'minun)

Leave them to think that their injustice is judgment and their ignorance is knowledge (their confusion, *ghamratihim*: their misguidance and ignorance).

I left them to their affairs, but until when?!

Until the Day of Resurrection!

{ So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day which they are promised } [Surat az-Zukhruf: 83]

So turn away from them completely, and busy yourself with the worship of your Lord, your work, your knowledge, and your life; they do not deserve the waste of time, nor thought, nor feeling.

Your deferred revenge:

And what is deferred victory but deferred revenge on those who were hostile to you?! { So leave them until they meet their Day in which they will be struck insensible } [Surat at-Tur: 45]

{ And whoever is patient and forgives — indeed, that is of the matters [requiring] determination }

[Surat ash-Shura: 43]

Forgiveness is understandable, because the verses came in the context of the wronged taking their right, so what about patience?

Because pardon requires abundant patience in swallowing anger, and pulling your impulses back, while you have the ability to take revenge and pursue justice forward!

Letting go is a choice and a decision, because you have the same ability and the same choice to punish!

They are two options equal for you in ability and capacity (pardon = justice)

"I once heard someone say to me: it is as if you are "allowing it," without actually allowing it." (Mel Robbins)

Think how many times you thought about punishment: I'll definitely do this, and I'll definitely say that.

Then,

you don't do,

anything.

How much time you've wasted in thinking and frying your nerves! And in the end you choose letting go, because you know it is the wisest, the most mature, the most complete, the best protection for you from the slips of childish behavior like flaring up and anger, which do not suit a mature person like you !

Tell me: when was the last time you got angry, fried your nerves, and this approach worked?! Very rarely.

Do you remember when you sent a Facebook message in which you blew up, raging with anger? And a voice message in which you flew off the handle with noise?

Tell me, for God's sake: didn't you regret it, even a little!

Admit it, for admitting is a good feeling that makes us take conscious decisions, and better ones in the future.

For the grudge before letting go and the anger after it are all of no use, a waste of time and a squandering of the standing you hold.
