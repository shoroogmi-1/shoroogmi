Title: Sun and Moon
Excerpt: A reflection on the complementary light of the Quran and Sunnah in the guidance of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

“...and as one who invites to Allah by His permission, and an illuminating lamp.”

You may describe someone as a moon—yes.
You may describe someone as a sun, or half a sun—yes.
But to describe someone as both sun and moon? No!

The sun and moon have not come together for anyone as they did for Muhammad ﷺ: “And the sun and moon are brought together.”

The moon is the Quran he brought, leading people from darkness into light. Whoever lives with the Quran alone is like someone living beneath the full moon alone, never seeing the day. The affairs of this world and religion are not complete without the purified Prophetic Sunnah, just as night is completed by day.

The Quran is the night, and the Sunnah completes the day and night.
Is the Quran not recited at night?
The full moon never disappears from the one who recites it, while other things may leave him surrounded by darkness.

The Sunnah is the sun through which the affairs of worldly life and religion are completed. Whoever holds fast to both has gained an abundant share.

“Blessed is He who placed constellations in the sky and placed in it a lamp and a shining moon.”

O Allah, send Your blessings and peace upon our Prophet Muhammad.
