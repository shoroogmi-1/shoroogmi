Title: Reflecting on the Words of Allah the Exalted (So be patient. Indeed, the promise of Allah is truth. And let them not disquiet you who are not certain [in faith])
Excerpt: The meaning: Allah’s promise to grant you victory over them is true, beyond any doubt

The meaning: Allah’s promise to grant you victory over them is true, beyond any doubt

*And let them not disquiet you*: let no one who lacks certainty in this promise provoke you, or sweep you into lightness and rashness.

## Reflection

To answer provocation in a rush of anger is a sign of thin patience, and it is one of the things that delays victory. For in doing so, a person spends his time and squanders his effort on retorts and outbursts, and his attention drifts from the true means of victory: righteous deeds, trust in Allah, and the like.
