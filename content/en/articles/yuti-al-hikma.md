Title: (Satan Threatens You with Poverty) Then (He Gives Wisdom to Whom He Wills)
Excerpt: (Satan threatens you with poverty …) and right after it comes (He gives wisdom to whom He wills..)

(Satan threatens you with poverty …) and right after it comes (He gives wisdom to whom He wills..)

Wisdom: the Quran, and the scholars agreed that what is meant by it here is understanding the Quran — its abrogating and abrogated verses, and its meanings.

And what my own reflection has led me to is this: the Quran — recited, memorized and understood, and above all read and heard explained in its exegesis — shields a person from the whispers of Satan, the whims of the self, and immorality in all its forms: stinginess, greed, insults, cursing and the like.

So whoever wants guidance, and wants to reach the very summit of happiness, let him hold fast to the Quran: set aside a daily portion and never abandon it, then look at the richness in your heart, and how it turns away from desires and sins. Fortify your heart with it at the start of the day, not at its end.
