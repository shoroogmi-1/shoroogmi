Title: Whoever Knows Allah, What Does He Not Know?
Excerpt: "Whoever knows Allah, what does he not know?

"Whoever knows Allah, what does he not know?

And whoever does not know Allah, what does he know?"

To set out across the land, leaving behind the various books and conflicting studies, and to walk out of your library with your hair disheveled, searching for knowledge with your heart, toward every heart, is a kind of knowledge.

Rivalry in increase distracts you: some scholars said it is the abundance of books.

“When you hear a hadith from me that your hearts recognize, and your hair and skin soften toward it, and you see that it is close to you, then I am the closest of you to it; and when you hear a hadith from me that your hearts reject, and your hair and skin recoil from it, and you see that it is far from you, then I am the farthest of you from it”

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ knew that hadiths would multiply and branches of knowledge would extend and not shrink; so he made the knowledge of the heart one of the methods of knowledge, for faith is a kind of knowledge recognized by people of reason.

And the strength of the heart's knowledge is according to the strength of faith and its branches, certainty and its shades, awe, humility, and lowliness and modesty before the truth and before people.

So worship and faith are the water the heart drinks, and from it then come forth the knowledge of words, deeds, and states.
