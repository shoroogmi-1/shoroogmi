Title: <em>Karāmah</em>
Excerpt: <em>Karāmah</em>, the wonder granted to the righteous, is not that He folds up for you the distances of the earth, the lands in all their length and breadth.

<em>Karāmah</em>, the wonder granted to the righteous, is not that He folds up for you the distances of the earth, the lands in all their length and breadth.

No, <em>karāmah</em> is that He folds up for you the distance between the heart and the Lord: He lifts from it the veils that hold you back, cleanses it of the intoxication of union, clears away the barriers to understanding, erases the images the world has stamped upon you, and brings you better than them, or their like (Do you not know that Allah is over all things competent)?

And He overpowers your wills with His will (and said to it and to the earth, “Come [into being], willingly or by compulsion.” They said, “We have come willingly”). He refers to the heaven with a pronoun of absence (<em>lahā</em>, “to it”), and the meaning is that even the humblest of creations, the earth, obeyed; He mentions it to remind you! And at that, a person sees how small he is.

So when the rescue of His gentleness reaches you, and the clouds of His mercy gather into view, His will overcomes your wills; and even if you do not want Him, He wants you (and because Allah guides whom He intends)

O you, if this is not love, then what is?
