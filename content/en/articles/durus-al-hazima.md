Title: Lessons of Defeat and Brokenness (Reflections on the Verses of Al Imran 2)
Excerpt: (Here you are loving them but they are not loving you)

(Here you are loving them but they are not loving you)

Allah marvels at the believers’ love for the hypocrites! And all the while the hypocrites hate them!

“In it is a marvel at the two states taken together: the wonder is that the believers love them while they hate the believers.”

So do not give your love to one who hates you.

(And Allah had certainly fulfilled His promise to you ..) He promised you victory at Uhud, but the promise was bound to obedience; when you disobeyed, there came the breaking and the defeat. Victory comes only through obedience.

No victory lasts forever!

Victory at the start of the battle, then pride, then the fall

A great victory turns into a crushing defeat the moment disobedience creeps in

(Then after distress, He sent down upon you security [in the form of] drowsiness, overcoming a faction of you, while another faction worried about themselves) Even in defeat and calamity, the believer holds a wondrous calm, while the hypocrite is consumed by worry for himself: terror keeps him from sleep

What do you do after a battle you have lost?

You go on —with the blessing of Allah— to the next battle

(Those [believers] who responded to Allah and the Messenger after injury had struck them) Despite the wounds they had taken at Uhud, as they were making their way back to Medina, the Messenger ﷺ ordered them out to Hamra al-Asad, and they answered and obeyed.

The Battle of Uhud: on a Saturday

And Hamra al-Asad: on the Sunday, in Shawwal

Not “I’ll come back tomorrow,” or “in four months,”

“Let me learn my lessons first!”

No! That is not how failure is met. Learning from failure happens in the moment: now, as it strikes, and then you march to battle the very next day.

One night to take in the lessons, to analyze and reflect, and tomorrow morning you put them into practice

The illusion of the enemy: the illusion of their power and might. This is Satan’s work: he whispers into your chest to frighten you and sap your resolve, and then, when you meet them, you find them to be nothing, so long as you are God-fearing and a believer (That is only Satan who frightens [you] of his supporters. So fear them not, but fear Me, if you are [indeed] believers) “So if he tempts you and fills you with illusions, rely on Me and take refuge in Me, for I am sufficient for you and will give you victory over them” - Ibn Kathir

And there is nothing better than to say: Sufficient for us is Allah, and [He is] the best Disposer of affairs.
