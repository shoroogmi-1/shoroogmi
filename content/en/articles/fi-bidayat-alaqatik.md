At the beginning of your relationship with the Quran…

Listen to a surah you love, in the voice of a reciter you love — whether or not he has mastered tajweed, because the aim here is to build love between you and the Quran.

When a problem confronts you, or a thought nags at your mind and you want to be sure of it, open the Mushaf at random and see how it hands you the answer you need, whatever the problem — I know there is a juristic disagreement over this practice (seeking a good omen in the Quran), and I do not want to enter into it; but once again, the aim here is to forge a first bond with the Quran.

Set yourself a portion of the Quran at the very start of your day, and never abandon it or put it off, whatever comes, for it is your impregnable fortress, the one you take with you to meet the toil and weariness of the day. Do not push it to the end of the day, after you have already taken so many blows! Prevention is better than cure, and this fortress gives you, by Allah's leave, strength, good hope, protection and care for the whole day; you feel companionship and richness filling your heart, and you feel that everything else the day brings, its provision and its people, is an extra, nothing more!
