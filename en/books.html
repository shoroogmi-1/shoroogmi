Ya Bushra Hadha Ghulam

Reflections on Thinking Well of Allah, Certainty, and Glad Tidings

Al-Ahsa Writers Association · In Arabic · 2026

I know of no book I have written from my heart, mixed with my blood, like (Ya Bushra Hadha Ghulam: Reflections on Thinking Well of Allah, Certainty, and Glad Tidings). I believe these three meanings are important pillars of faith for deepening our relationship with a Merciful, Loving Lord, not a passing optimistic mood.

I published the book electronically, and it met with an appreciation and a reach I did not expect, and by the grace of Allah I saw the effect of its words on those around me. We have now printed it in a new paper edition, organized, indexed and proofread, and added new articles to it that I found satisfying and worthy of my dear reader.

The Encyclopedia of Connective Tafsir

Connections between surahs, between the topics of a surah, and between its verses — Surat al-Baqarah

Research: AI · Reviewed by: Shoroog Almehmadi · In Arabic · 2026

Why does this verse come after that one? Why does this topic follow the one before it? A colorful illustrated book gathering the connections of Surat al-Baqarah at three levels: the surah's link to the surahs before and after it, the links between its topics, and the connection between each verse and the next.

Every connection in it comes from a scholar's own text, quoted word for word and attributed to its author: al-Biqa'i's Nazm al-Durar, Ibn Ashur's al-Tahrir wa al-Tanwir, Abu Hayyan's al-Bahr al-Muhit, and other works of tafsir.
