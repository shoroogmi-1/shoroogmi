Honor Your Children and Perfect Their Manners

  • 📅 August 3, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

“From the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, who said: Honor your children and perfect their manners.”

His saying: (Honor your children), for honoring them increases their love for their parents; and since honoring may lead to bad manners, he indicated by his saying (and perfect their manners) that honoring should not go that far.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
