Fame & Celebrities

On Campaigns to Boost Followers

  • 📅 September 22, 2026
  • ⏱️ 3 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

To those who message me privately (with campaigns to boost followers and likes)

- There is a scholarly consensus, as far as I know, that trading in followers, buying them or selling them, is forbidden; for in doing so you deceive your followers: people take them for real followers when they are a phantom audience. (So seek out another source of income, lawful and wholesome; I am truly a sincere adviser to you)

- And then, my dear ones: by Allah, I spent three years studying fame and wrote a study on it (Fame and Celebrities: A Study of the System, Forms, Process, and Possible Theory); I read sixty English books on public relations, the very field that makes stars and celebrities, and wrote a book on it; my bachelor's degree is in public relations, and I studied marketing for three months… None of this is to show off! It is to tell you, my son, my daughter: I have the skill and the knowledge in this field (fame), but not the desire! I do not want a phantom audience. A person, I believe, once fully mature, cannot fool himself by letting his soul live on rosy illusions of fame, manufactured stardom, and counterfeit praise, only to find, when he reaches for the reality, nothing but ashes. And I cannot bear even half a second, or a quarter of one, of living in psychological, mental, and social self-deception. This is a sickness! This is madness! A pestilent breeding ground of cursed, lying deception.

- Finally, we all have the natural longing for a crowd and a little shine. But every time my own soul wants to take the crooked paths here and there to win fame and spread its name, I remind it of the permissible ways, for they are the best, the purest, and the most God-fearing: making content and organizing it; “natural” marketing, public relations, press, and advertising campaigns that grow a real audience steadily and unhurriedly, with nothing wrong in them; and walking slowly, step by step, instead of the sudden, rapid growth that throws mental and social functioning into disarray and does more harm than good.

- And even so, for all that I do, there are those who impugn my intentions and my direction -and I keep silent and do not trade word for word- for some have said to me: you fame-seeker, you who are empty of meaning! Some hint at showing off! And some send me long, sprawling sermons on sincerity in private, and I know exactly what they are driving at; yet I meet it all by ignoring, pardoning, and letting it go. And I say to myself: this, too, is a trial!

I know that some of my dear ones do not agree with me in this approach, and may see in it a harshness toward oneself; but the path has become clear to me in this matter. For every dilemma, Allah has given us a clear and healing guidance that leads us along the plainest road and the soundest way, and, most important of all, the middle one.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
