Fame & Celebrities

On Follower-Boosting Campaigns

  • 📅 September 22, 2026
  • ⏱️ 3 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

As for the people who message me privately (campaigns to boost followers and likes)

- There is a juristic consensus -as far as I know- on the prohibition of trading by selling or buying followers; because by this you deceive your followers, and people think they are real followers when they are a fake audience. (So look for another lawful, wholesome source of income; I am a sincere adviser to you)

- And then, my dear ones: by Allah, I studied fame for three years and wrote a study on it (Fame and Celebrities: A Study of the System, Forms, Process, and Possible Theory ) and read sixty English books on public relations and wrote a book on it, and it is the field that makes stars and celebrities, and my bachelor's major is public relations, and I studied marketing for three months… And what I'm doing is not showing off my abilities! Rather, it's to tell you: my son, my daughter: I have the skill and the knowledge in this field (fame), but I don't have the desire! I don't want fake audiences, and a person, I think, once his maturity is complete, cannot deceive himself by letting it live rosy illusions of fame, manufactured stardom, and fake praise, then find nothing of its reality but ashes. And I cannot bear half a second and a quarter of living psychological, mental, and social deception. This is a sickness! This is madness! An epidemic environment of damned, false deception.

- Finally, we all have the natural desire for an audience and to shine, except that every time my self wants to take the crooked roads here and there to become famous and have its name spread, I remind it of the permissible ways, for they are the best, the purest, and the most God-fearing: like making content, organizing it, and "natural" marketing, public relations, press, and advertising campaigns that grow a real audience steadily and patiently, with nothing wrong in them, and walking slowly and gradually instead of rapid, sudden growth that disrupts mental and social functions, whose harm is greater than its benefit.

- And with all that I do, there are those who question my intention and my orientation -and I stay silent and don't answer word with word- for there are those who have said to me: you seeker of fame, you who are empty of meaning, and there are those who hint to me about showing off! And there are those who send long, broad admonitions about sincerity privately, and I know what they are getting at, except that I deal with it by ignoring, pardoning, and overlooking. And I say to myself: this too is a trial!

I know that some of my dear ones do not agree with me on this approach and may see in it being too hard on oneself, except that the way has become clear to me in this matter. Allah has made clear to us, in every dilemma, a healing clarification that guides us on the clearest path and the soundest approach, and most importantly: its middle.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
