Fame & Celebrities

Thank God I Am Not Famous

  • 📅 August 7, 2026
  • ⏱️ 2 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

How often has fame left injustice in its wake: a person is drawn into some quarrel, and either he is wronged or he wrongs.

Or he rises to fame, is loved, gathers admirers across the earth, and then fame leaves him a single slip, filmed and passed from hand to hand; he is consumed with regret and sorrow, and cast aside the way carcasses are cast aside.

And there is fame built on ignorance, so that all he does and says is ignorant; however many millions it brings in, it will soon wither away.

Fame may come to someone through a scandal (we ask Allah the Exalted for well-being), so praise Him that He has covered us and kept us safe, hidden us from people's eyes, and drawn a veil over our faults.

Soaring fame in his youth, the numbers, the money; then, in his mid-thirties, a thunderous fall: the world curses him and the numbers dwindle, he who was once the toast of everyone. And so his story is sealed with a death before death.

Or perhaps his standing among people stayed high his whole life long, and only after death was he exposed and disgraced, so that his fame became a curse upon him. I ask Allah, for us and for you, a covering at death, after death and before death, and a sincere repentance before the chance is gone.

And how often has soaring fame left behind regret, sorrow, crushing disappointment, and a gloom that imprisons a person until he cries out: "Oh, I wish that I were dust!"

These are scenes that weigh upon the heart, I know. But I set them before you so that you may see how blessed you are, lose your appetite for the spotlight, and come to see obscurity as a great grace.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
