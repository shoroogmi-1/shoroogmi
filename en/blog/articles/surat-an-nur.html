Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

Surat an-Nur Explains to Us How to Reach the Light

  • 📅 August 10, 2026
  • ⏱️ 14 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

Reaching the light of Allah in the heart of His believing servant does not require a great deal of prayer and fasting; the matter is easier than that. These three are enough for you:

1- Purifying the hearing

2- Purifying the tongue

3- Purifying the sight

And the light will come, if Allah wills!

The surah begins with only two verses about the well-known physical indecency (verses 2-3), and 23 verses about the kinds of verbal indecency! (4-26)

To tell us that verbal indecency is greater than we imagine; we take it lightly, but Allah says it is grave!

(When you received it with your tongues and said with your mouths that of which you had no knowledge, and you thought it was a light matter, while with Allah it is tremendous (15) [An-Nur]

The kinds of verbal indecency in the surah:

1- Accusing chaste, unsuspecting women, and likewise chaste, unsuspecting believing men, without evidence or witnesses.

2- Spouses accusing each other without evidence or witnesses.

3- The lying slander and fabrication against people's honor while they are entirely innocent.

4- Thinking ill of believing men and women and accusing them regarding their honor without proof or witness, merely out of whim; and this Allah the Exalted called a manifest slander.

5- Delving into people's honor even if there is proof and evidence and what happened did happen. Are you a judge? Or are you a witness with evidence? The answer is no! If so, then why do you delve into their honor even if it happened and the news is true? What business is it of yours? Mind your own self and weep over your own sin. We have known people who delved into the honor of others, gloating and mocking, and then fell into the very thing they had fallen into. So take heed and beware; these are not the manners of one who comes to his Lord with a sound heart.

"By Allah, O Hazzal, if you had covered him with your garment, it would have been better than what you did to him" Narrated by Imam Ahmad

Imam Malik said:

"I knew in this town - meaning Madinah - people who had no faults, but they found fault with others, so they came to have faults; and I knew in this town people who had faults, but they kept silent about the faults of others, so their faults were forgotten"

So cover the faults of Muslim men and women, and Allah will cover yours in this world and the Hereafter.

And the remedy is in what the verse mentioned (And why, when you heard it, did you not say: It is not for us to speak of this. Exalted are You; this is a great slander.) [An-Nur]

The meaning: why, when you heard such news, did you not stay silent and not delve into it, and say: Glory be to You! This is false talk; it is not for us to let our tongues speak of it, or let our suspicions turn over it, or mention it to anyone. Glory be to You, our Lord, we declare You free of this enormous lie!

Ibn Kathir said: (This is another lesson in manners after the first, the command to think well: that is, when something unbecoming is said about the best of people, then all the more should one think well of them, and not let himself feel anything other than that; then if something of that clings to his soul -a whisper or an imagining- he should not speak of it).

So the verses made clear the purifying of the hearing from listening to such rumors and news -even if true- and that one should not browse them or comment on them, but pass by them with dignity, because these are the qualities of the servants of the Most Merciful (and when they pass by ill speech, they pass by with dignity); and the purifying of the tongue and of suspicions, by mentioning believing men and women only with good and thinking well of them.

6- Swearing a lot! That a person should not swear often, and should lighten himself of idle oaths as much as he can; it is disliked, and some counted it among the traits of hypocrisy. My father told us that he has a friend who has gone many years without swearing by Allah even once! I said: That is true honor!

(And let not those of virtue among you and wealth swear..) (An-Nur)

Lā yaʾtali: let him not swear.

7- Pardoning and overlooking whoever slandered others' honor, if he repents, turns back and does not persist in what he said (And let not those of virtue and means among you swear not to give to relatives, the needy and those who emigrated in the cause of Allah; let them pardon and overlook. Would you not love that Allah should forgive you? And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful (22)

The result:

(Wicked women are for wicked men, and wicked men are for wicked women; and good women are for good men, and good men are for good women. Those are declared innocent of what they say; for them is forgiveness and noble provision.) [An-Nur]

The more a person purifies himself and cleanses himself of wicked speech and purifies his hearing and tongue, the purer and more chaste he becomes, and the more deserving of chastity and goodness (those whom the angels take in death, being good). They made their speech and hearing good, free of filth and of attacking people's honor, so Allah made their words good for them at the time of death; and these are the ones who deserve to be described as good. And this goodness is what qualifies him for light to enter the depths of his heart and his inner self, even if he was not striving in a great deal of prayer and fasting

“They said: O Messenger of Allah, so-and-so fasts by day and prays at night, but she harms her neighbors? He said: She is in the Fire. They said: O Messenger of Allah, so-and-so prays the obligatory prayers and gives in charity pieces of dried curd, and she does not harm her neighbors? He said: She is in Paradise” - Sahih

And in it is proof that whoever thinks ill of people, and delves into their honor, and hears it, believes it and repeats it, is wicked, a wicked man or a wicked woman.

"A proof that Allah, Glorified and Exalted is He, clears the family of a good, chaste man of wickedness; because good women are for good men and good men for good women. And this is part of the wisdom of Allah the Mighty and Majestic: the more a person is good, clean and pure, the more Allah, Glorified and Exalted is He, prepares for him a family of the same standing, a fitting recompense; and the matter is likewise the reverse if he were wicked, especially in what relates to chastity. And this is what is usually seen in reality: that a man of wicked character has a family like that, because he did not protect himself, so Allah the Mighty and Majestic did not protect him"

(al-Tafsir al-Muharrar)

"Wicked words are for wicked men, and wicked men are for wicked words; and good words are for good men, and good men are for good words. That is: the wicked words the hypocrites attributed to Aisha are more fitting for them, and she is more fitting for innocence and purity than they are; and so the Exalted said: Those are declared innocent of what they say [An-Nur: 26]," (Ibn Kathir).

And part of protecting the hearing, tongue and sight is that He laid down the etiquette of asking permission to enter, so that one's eye does not fall on what he would dislike of his own family and of people.

And Allah the Exalted does not only forbid the act itself; He forbids merely approaching it (And do not approach this tree) (And do not approach the orphan's property..) and it is abrogated, (And do not approach unlawful sexual intercourse..).

(And do not approach this tree) He emphasized the prohibition and made it stronger by first telling them not to approach it, and then forbidding them the act itself (eating).

Because merely approaching it, seeing it and hearing it makes the sin attractive in the heart and makes a person love it.

He forbade approaching, and any cause that leads to sin He forbade, and a person becomes one who wrongs himself if he approaches it, merely approaches it

"Nearness to a thing gives rise to an urge and an inclination that seizes the whole heart; it is what familiarity entails, and familiarity is an urge to love, so one sees nothing ugly and hears no prohibition, and it distracts him from what revelation and reason require; for He made their approaching the tree a cause for their being among the wrongdoers, and the cause that invites to evil is forbidden, just as the cause that leads to good is commanded" (al-Tafsir al-Muharrar)

3- Purifying the sight: He commanded believing men and women to lower their gaze, for it is a cause of attaining goodness and chastity.

And He commanded believing women with etiquettes that help purify sight, speech and hearing: as for hearing, they should not strike their feet to make known what they conceal of their adornment: that is, a woman striking the anklet on her foot so that a man hears it; and He commanded her to wear hijab, and in it is purification of the sight.

And He commanded whoever cannot find the means to marry to keep chaste and pure until Allah enriches him and provides for him. And the means of chastity he must exert and strive in are: much fasting, lowering the gaze and what was mentioned earlier, and to busy himself with knowledge and beneficial hobbies away from thinking about this matter, and not to occupy his mind with it much. Haven't you heard that Imam al-Nawawi was asked: When will you marry? And he said: I forgot about it altogether! Meaning, it's not on my mind, because he was so busy with knowledge. And Ibn Kathir said about the saying of the Exalted (And let those who find not the means to marry keep themselves chaste until Allah enriches them from His bounty)

it is the man who has no wife: "let him look into the dominion of the heavens and the earth until Allah enriches him."!

So if he does all these things and purifies his hearing, sight and tongue and keeps chaste, his heart is purified and becomes more fit for the light of Allah to enter his heart. And what is the light of Allah? It is the Quran!

(And We have certainly sent down to you clarifying verses, and an example of those who passed on before you, and an admonition for the God-fearing. (34) Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth. The likeness of His light is that of a niche in which is a lamp; the lamp is in a glass; the glass is as if it were a brilliant star, lit from a blessed olive tree, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil would almost glow even if no fire touched it. Light upon light. Allah guides to His light whom He wills, and Allah sets forth parables for people, and Allah is All-Knowing of everything. (35))

Admonition: a deterrent from sin.

So emptying comes before adorning: when one has emptied himself, purified and cleansed himself of the causes of verbal and physical indecency and not approached them, he is most deserving of the light of Allah and of taking hold of the Quran.

And when the light settled in his heart, he became a spreader of light everywhere: (In houses which Allah has permitted to be raised and in which His name is remembered; glorifying Him therein in the mornings and the evenings are men whom neither trade nor sale distracts from the remembrance of Allah, establishing prayer and giving zakah; they fear a Day in which hearts and eyes will be turned about) (An-Nur)

The verses show the effect of this light: that the believer then prefers the Hereafter over this world, and his heart becomes fearful, humble and submissive to Allah the Exalted, and fear, awe and hope settle in it. And these lofty qualities are attained only in this way! Purity of hearing, sight and tongue, then the light of Allah, the Quran, until he reaches the fruits of the light, and spreading the light among people (And is one who was dead and We gave him life and made for him a light by which to walk among the people) and this light is the Quran; his heart was illuminated by it, then it shone for those around him.

So the believer's light is internal, real and rooted, from his heart, and then it lights up what is around him; while the hypocrite's light is external, with no root in the heart, as the verses of Surat al-Baqarah show (Their example is that of one who kindled a fire, but when it illuminated what was around him) the hypocrite sought someone else's help to kindle the light, and it lit up the outside, not his heart, as in al-Tafsir al-Muharrar.

Then He showed the state of the one who is the opposite of that: (Or like darknesses within a deep sea, covered by waves upon which are waves, over which are clouds; darknesses, some of them upon others. When one puts out his hand, he can hardly see it. And he to whom Allah has not granted light, for him there is no light (40) (An-Nur)

Whoever's speech, hearing and sight are wicked, and who is the opposite of those qualities, is in darkness of heart on top of darkness of place, spreading wicked speech. So how would Allah grant light to one who did not take up the means of light!

And whoever reflects on Surat an-Nur finds in it synonyms of light!

Flash, lightning and sight (The flash of its lightning almost takes away the eyesight. Allah alternates the night and the day. Indeed in that is a lesson for those who have vision) so Allah is able to bring the servant out of darkness into light and the reverse, according to whether he takes up or abandons the means of light. And there are means of steadfastness the servant exerts so that his heart is not turned about as the night and day are turned about “A man will wake up a believer and by evening be a disbeliever, and a man will go to evening a believer and wake up a disbeliever, selling his religion for a small worldly gain”

Then He crowns this with obedience to Allah and His Messenger ﷺ and contentment with their rulings, while the hypocrite is the opposite of this; and that whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger and attains awe has attained success (And whoever obeys Allah and His Messenger and fears Allah and is mindful of Him, those are the successful ones (52) (An-Nur)

So when these stages are complete for him, Allah, by His permission, establishes him in the land:

(Allah has promised those of you who believe and do righteous deeds that He will surely make them successors in the land as He made those before them successors, and that He will surely establish for them their religion which He has approved for them, and that He will surely replace their fear with security; they worship Me, associating nothing with Me. And whoever disbelieves after that, those are the defiantly disobedient (55) (An-Nur)

And how could he be established in the land when he has not purified himself of foul speech and letting his gaze roam free? Because he will be in a position to be followed as an example, so these qualities do not befit him; there must be purification first, so that establishment can come after!

And notice here that He described whoever returns to darkness after being established in the land as defiantly disobedient! “Insulting a Muslim is defiant disobedience”.

Purifying the inner self through the hearing, the tongue and the sight; then light settles in his heart and he takes hold of the Quran, then he spreads the light, and stands firm in obedience to Allah and His Messenger ﷺ, and so he is established in the land -if Allah wills-.

And whoever reflects on the surah finds that it repeats often (And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing) (And Allah is All-Encompassing, All-Knowing), emphasizing that Allah hears your words, so beware of what you say and what you do, for He hears them and knows them; nothing of them is hidden from Him. And whoever is mindful of the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing receives a fitting recompense: All-Encompassing, All-Knowing, He expands for him in his religion and his worldly life, and increases him in light.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
