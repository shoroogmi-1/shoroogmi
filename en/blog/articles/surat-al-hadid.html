Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

An Exposition of Surat al-Hadid for the One Seeking More

  • 📅 August 21, 2026
  • ⏱️ 27 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

(Whatever is in the heavens and the earth exalts Allah, and He is the Almighty, the All-Wise (1) His is the dominion of the heavens and the earth; He gives life and causes death, and He has power over all things (2) He is the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He has knowledge of all things (3) It is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days, then rose over the Throne. He knows what enters into the earth and what comes out of it, and what descends from the sky and what ascends into it; and He is with you wherever you are, and Allah sees what you do (4) His is the dominion of the heavens and the earth, and to Allah all matters are returned (5) He merges the night into the day and merges the day into the night, and He knows what is within the breasts (6) (Al-Hadid)

These verses sum up, in their names and attributes, everything that will come after them in Surat al-Hadid!

Attributes of power: His is the dominion of the heavens and the earth; He gives life and causes death, and He has power over all things

Attributes of knowledge and awareness: He is the First and the Last, the First and the Last, the Manifest and the Hidden, and He has knowledge of all things

And His knowledge of the finest details of what is on the surface and in the depths of the earth and the sky

Then, once you know that, the theme of the surah takes shape and becomes clear: being mindful of Allah watching in private and in public is the cause of awe and humility (and He is with you wherever you are).

And to Allah all matters are returned: to Allah are raised the decrees, the deeds and the states, and He looks into their affairs and changes them; He guides the astray, forgives the sinners, and relieves the distressed.

The verses of faith in Allah and His Messenger and following the revealed Book came, and after them the verses of the conquest! To say that there is no conquest and victory without obeying Allah and His Messenger and those in authority, from scholars and rulers (And why do you not believe in Allah while the Messenger invites you to believe in your Lord, and He has taken your covenant, if you are believers (8) It is He who sends down upon His servant clear verses to bring you out of darkness into light; and indeed Allah is Kind and Merciful to you (9) And why do you not spend in the cause of Allah, while to Allah belongs the inheritance of the heavens and the earth? Not equal among you are those who spent before the conquest and fought; they are greater in rank than those who spent afterwards and fought. And to each Allah has promised the best, and Allah is Aware of what you do (10) Who is it that will lend Allah a goodly loan, so He will multiply it for him, and he will have a noble reward (11) (Al-Hadid)

The verse made clear that whoever spends in times of calamity and hardship is firmer in his faith when the world later opens up to him,

For protection from being tempted by the world comes only through spending before the conquest and after it, and how many have failed this test: they succeed in patience and fail in gratitude.

Because whoever spends before the conquest and after it, the two states are equal to him; he spends with certainty that Allah will compensate him with better, and because of his certainty of victory and spoils; while the other's certainty is weaker, so he is stingy and miserly, watching the news and events until, when the conquest comes, he spends!

And so spending is the test of the ṣiddīqūn (the truthful ones)

Indeed, the men who give in charity and the women who give in charity and have lent Allah a goodly loan, it will be multiplied for them, and they will have a noble reward (18) And those who believe in Allah and His messengers, it is they who are the truthful ones; and the martyrs, with their Lord, will have their reward and their light. But those who disbelieved and denied Our signs, they are the companions of Hellfire (19) (Al-Hadid)

He said al-muṣṣaddiqīn, not al-mutaṣaddiqīn, and in this is a subtle meaning: that the one who gives in charity is one who believes (muṣaddiq) that Allah will replace it for him with better and multiply it for him, and that this is what raises him to later be among the ṣiddīqūn; because the ṣiddīq is the one who believed Allah and His Messenger and their promises, and acted accordingly.

(On the Day you see the believing men and the believing women, their light running ahead of them and on their right: "Your good tidings today are gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding therein eternally; that is the great attainment" (12) On the Day the hypocrite men and the hypocrite women will say to those who believed, "Wait for us, that we may borrow from your light." It will be said, "Go back behind you and seek light." And a wall will be set up between them with a door; its inner side has mercy, and its outer side, facing it, has punishment (13) They will call to them, "Were we not with you?" They will say, "Yes, but you tempted yourselves, and you waited and doubted, and wishful thinking deluded you until the command of Allah came, and the Deceiver deceived you about Allah (14) So today no ransom will be taken from you, nor from those who disbelieved. Your refuge is the Fire; it is your patron, and wretched is the destination (15) (Al-Hadid)

The exegetes say: when the believers are on the Path together with the hypocrites, both groups have light, except that the hypocrite's light goes out; because it is an outward, external light with no reality to it, since he did not realize it in this world, so it has no reality in the Hereafter; and there the believers supplicate (they will say, "Our Lord, perfect for us our light") (At-Tahrim)

So whoever Allah has not given light from the Quran and the Sunnah in this world has no light and no share in the Hereafter (And he to whom Allah has not granted light, for him there is no light (40) (An-Nur)

And the one who holds most to the Quran and the Sunnah will have the greatest light on the Day of Resurrection.

You tempted yourselves: like the testing of iron when it is put to the fire, separating the impure from the pure; and so it is with hearts when they are put to the light of the Quran, for ambiguous matters are presented to them, and some of them deviate and some Allah guides; all this so that Allah may know who fears Him unseen.

You tempted yourselves with doubts, so you waited with desires, and you were bewildered in the darknesses and thought them many colors while their color is one and their ways are many, and wishful thinking deluded you until you were among the dead; so for your forgetting of the Hereafter, your patron is the Fire; it is their patron from this world because they took it as an ally through desires (the Fire is surrounded by desires), and by contrast it is understood that Paradise took the believer as its ally (We are your allies in the life of this world and in the Hereafter, and you will have therein whatever your souls desire, and you will have therein whatever you request (31) (Fussilat)

Because Paradise is surrounded by hardships; they fasted from desires in this world, so in return they were given what their souls desire in Paradise; a reward from your Lord, a sufficient gift.

Should the believer not race against time to perfect the light, and not let the world take him and make him forget the Hereafter?

(Has the time not come for those who have believed that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah and what has come down of the truth, and that they not be like those who were given the Scripture before, and a long period passed over them, so their hearts hardened; and many of them are defiantly disobedient (16) Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its death. We have made clear to you the signs, that you might reason (17) (Al-Hadid)

(Has the time not come) as though the believer were in a state of waiting and expecting a humility to descend upon him from the sky, and putting off the ascent of his heart up the stairways of faith; so his Lord says to him, in the gentlest address and the most delightful companionship: Is not now the right time for humility, as the verse fell upon your ears, O My servant? And when that seemed hard to him and weighed on him — how can I reach a lofty station that belongs to the scholars when I have no knowledge!? (Only those of His servants who have knowledge fear Allah) — He answered him with ease and facilitation (that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah and what has come down of the truth): that his heart be softened by the Quran, for He has made clear the disease and the cure; the disease of hardness, its cure is the Quran, softening it until it is humble.

And after gentleness and encouragement, He turned to warning, cautioning whoever goes back to postponing humility not to be like the People of the Book, over whom a long time passed without a Book, so their hearts hardened,

And I think the People of the Book meant here are the Jews; because they are the ones whose hearts are hard (Then your hearts hardened after that, so they are like stones..)

And at the end of Surat al-Hadid He spoke about the opposite state to hardness, which is the tenderness found in Christianity, and that Allah forbids resembling the People of the Book even in their states of heart, so that one has neither hardness of heart like the Jews nor the excessive tenderness that keeps them from establishing the prescribed punishments, as with the Christians -and its explanation will come in its place-.

(Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its death) so that you come to know that He is able to revive dead hearts through His remembrance, so do not think it unlikely that this happens, nor the guidance of the astray and the disbeliever until he becomes among the most fearful of people.

(We have made clear to you the signs, that you might reason) here He has made clear to you the head of the matter: fear of Allah; and that the fruit of knowledge is awe, and that nothing in it is obscure or difficult; so is there any person of reason who will take these words?

And in the verse is that Allah does not approve disbelief for His servants, and does not approve humiliation for His believing servant, nor little and weak faith; so He wanted to stir his heart to seek more faith and ascend its stairways, and not to be content with the lower rank of it (And you will be superior if you are believers) (Al Imran).

And He placed around the verse of the heart's humility two verses about the world! Above it and below it, on the right page and on the left, like a flowing river engulfing it and besieging it (and wishful thinking deluded you…) on the right page, (Know that the life of this world is but play and amusement and adornment..) on the left page.

(Know that the life of this world is but play and amusement and adornment and boasting among you and competition in increase of wealth and children — like the example of a rain whose plants please the tillers; then it dries and you see it turned yellow; then it becomes debris. And in the Hereafter is severe punishment, and forgiveness from Allah and approval. And what is the life of this world except the enjoyment of delusion (20) Race toward forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden whose width is like the width of the heaven and the earth, prepared for those who believed in Allah and His messengers. That is the bounty of Allah which He gives to whom He wills, and Allah is the possessor of great bounty (21) (Al-Hadid)

He turned from awe and humility, in which time is held still and slowed, to making clear the reason they do not attain such states, namely racing in the world and chasing after it; and the whole Quran describes the world briefly: play and amusement, except here in Surat al-Hadid, where He detailed it more (play and amusement and adornment and boasting among you and competition in increase of wealth and children). He is saying that the cause of hardness of heart and distance from the remembrance of Allah is preoccupation with wealth and children, and this is the trial that turns the heart away from the Quran and remembrance (And know that your wealth and your children are but a trial and that Allah has with Him a great reward (28) (Al-Anfal). So He added to their knowledge two more pieces of knowledge; as for competing in increase and scattering, this is what distracted them from remembering the Hereafter and worship (Competition in increase diverted you until you visited the graves). Then came the completion of the advice (O you who have believed, let not your wealth and your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah. And whoever does that, it is they who are the losers (9) (Al-Munafiqun)

(O you who have believed, when you meet a company, stand firm and remember Allah much, that you may be successful (45)

So if this fighter, amid war and blood and severed heads, was commanded to remember Allah, and not to let the scenes of death distract him from remembrance! And that the firmness of his foot and his victory lie in remembrance. Had Allah exempted anyone from remembrance, He would have exempted the fighter!

So what of one who is busy with buying and trade?

(And when the prayer has been concluded, disperse in the land and seek from the bounty of Allah, and remember Allah much, that you may succeed (10)

And when they saw a transaction or a diversion, they rushed to it and left you standing. Say, "What is with Allah is better than diversion and than a transaction, and Allah is the best of providers" (11) (Al-Jumu'ah) And He praised a people whom the world did not distract from His remembrance (Men whom neither commerce nor sale distracts from the remembrance of Allah and performance of prayer and giving of zakah. They fear a Day in which the hearts and eyes will turn about (37) (An-Nur) And once again He makes clear to us that the cause of fearing Allah unseen and being in awe of Him is remembering Him with the heart, the tongue and deeds.

There, when time stands still and movement settles: if a verse stirred your heart and spread longing, then stop! And repeat it until the tears flow and the soul ascends the stairways of humility; and the last does not happen to one who ignores a verse that stirred his heart, his concern being the end of the surah. And what harm would it do you if you fell behind in your daily portion because there is a verse that softened your heart? This is better for you than gathering gold and silver in the old crucibles.

And part of the understanding of the righteous was that when their eyes wept from awe, they wiped their tears and spread them over the whole face, so that the whole face would not be touched by the Fire! (Sifat al-Safwah).

A dead heart revived by humility, and a heart that was alive killed by the world (like the example of a rain whose plants please the tillers; then it dries and you see it turned yellow; then it becomes debris) the Quran had been its spring, then when the world came loose upon it, it made it forget the Quran and night prayer; so it reverted to its first state of hardness and heedlessness, and the calamity returned to it.

(No calamity strikes upon the earth or among yourselves except that it is in a register before We bring it into being. Indeed that, for Allah, is easy (22) In order that you not despair over what has eluded you and not exult over what He has given you. And Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful (23) Those who are stingy and enjoin upon people stinginess. And whoever turns away, then indeed Allah is the Free of need, the Praiseworthy (24) (Al-Hadid)

And the one who reflects finds that the right page facing this verse is, on the left page, (Know that Allah gives life to the earth after its death…)

(In it is an indication that He, the Exalted, softens hearts after their hardness, and guides the bewildered after their straying, and relieves distress after its severity) (Ibn Kathir)

Decreeing the calamity is easy for Him, and lifting it is easy for Him. And when He pointed out that the difficult is made easy, He pointed out the pitfalls of relief!

Swaggering in one's walk and being deluded by the world, and it made him forget that it is a trial; and delusion and boasting lead to stinginess in spending, so they withhold wealth, and use their standing to command people to be stingy! For he has reached the most extreme state of miserliness and greed

That he commands people to be stingy! And this is undoubtedly a trait of the hypocrite (they enjoin what is wrong).

And among the wisdoms of adversity is expelling impurity and softening the hard heart (Then why, when Our punishment came to them, did they not humble themselves? But their hearts became hardened) (Al-An'am) So whoever's heart hardens in calamity has increased the adversity upon himself, and whoever is severe, Allah is severe with him.

(We have already sent Our messengers with clear proofs and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance that the people may maintain justice. And We sent down iron, wherein is great might and benefits for the people, and so that Allah may know who supports Him and His messengers unseen. Indeed, Allah is Powerful and Almighty (25) And We have already sent Noah and Abraham and placed in their descendants prophethood and scripture; and among them is he who is guided, but many of them are defiantly disobedient (26) Al-Hadid

The one who reflects on the Quran notices that after verses commanding justice, the messengers and the scriptures are mentioned immediately; to point out that the Shariah is the best way to achieve justice in the self and in society, as in An-Nisa: (..be persistently upholders of justice…) and after it (Believe in Allah and His Messenger and the Book that He sent down upon His Messenger..) and Al-An'am (And when you speak, be just, even if it concerns a near relative…) and after it (And this is My path, which is straight, so follow it..) and it is repeated in Surat al-Hadid as well.

(and sent down with them the Scripture and the balance that the people may maintain justice) He paired the Book with justice, so whoever takes hold of the Book establishes justice even against himself, and becomes just in his character, balanced in his temperament, moderate in all his affairs, his private self the same as his public self, nothing at odds between them; just! (O you who have believed, be persistently upholders of justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents and relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. So follow not desire, lest you not be just. And if you distort or refuse, then indeed Allah is ever, of what you do, Aware (135) (An-Nisa) He called them by the name of faith, for nothing befits the believer but justice toward himself and his society; it does not befit him to lean with whims and turn away from the truth once it has become clear to him.

(that the people may maintain justice) He omitted the object (in what do they maintain justice?) so that it is unrestricted and general, even in their dealings with tiny ants and grasses. And He revealed to a prophet: “Why not just one ant” that you burned? And in fiqh it is disliked to tread on green meadows with one's foot or one's mount without need.

(And Allah is greater in might) (An-Nisa) He is severe in might in punishment and retribution, and it also includes that His might is severe in reviving hearts that have rusted; and when the Chosen One (peace be upon him) witnessed hearts changing between disbelief and faith, his most frequent oath was “No, by the Turner of hearts” because he witnessed the power after knowing it. His might is severe even in guidance and mercy. So that creation turns to Him seeking guidance from Him alone, for He is the One able to grant it and the Subjugator above His servants, and so they do not rely on their educational and psychological methods alone, even though they are means that should be taken.

(and so that Allah may know who supports Him and His messengers unseen) If you establish the religion, you have supported Allah and His messengers, and He qualified both with "unseen"! Because the true establishment of the religion is in the unseen, in hiding and within, where no one sees it “O Allah, I ask You for fear of You in the unseen and the seen”

And the greatest victory achieved in the unseen by a believer is that he fears Allah and sheds tears in solitude far from people

(Has the time not come for those who have believed that their hearts should become humbly submissive at the remembrance of Allah)

And he commands himself to do right and forbids himself from wrong, then his society; not the reverse!

(And We sent down iron) And when hearts thought their change unlikely, and souls found it impossible to imagine being turned from their natures, and because some souls despaired of being set right and turned away, He reminded them with a fiery example: the heart is like iron; does it not, when smelted in fire, melt and its properties change? From it are made pots and vessels after it had nearly rusted and lost its worth (And We certainly gave David from Us bounty. [We said], "O mountains, repeat praises with him, and the birds." And We made pliable for him iron (10) [Commanding him], "Make full coats of mail and calculate the links, and work righteousness. Indeed I, of what you do, am Seeing" (11) (Saba)

Indeed, the One who softened iron is able, through the Quran, to soften hearts by guiding them after straying, humbling them after their roughness, uniting them after division, and setting right the heart of one who has despaired and lost hope of setting himself right.

(And We made pliable) He attributed the softening of iron to Allah the Exalted and used the "We" of majesty; because iron is greatly hard, and none can soften it but its Creator; so just as He softened iron for David (peace be upon him), He is able to soften hearts and set souls right by His bounty, mercy and generosity.

I had wondered why Allah did not mention David (peace be upon him) in Surat al-Hadid!? And mentioned Noah and Abraham (peace be upon them)? The answer may be that the iron here is the figurative, not the material: softening the heart, and faith and humility through constant mindfulness (and He is with you wherever you are).

Striving, spiritual exercises and purification of hearts, if they are not upon the guidance of the Quran and the Sunnah, have no effect.

And for this reason, after it He warned against innovations, extremism and harshness in matters of faith, as the Christians did; they had states of faith, with softness and tenderness in their hearts, except that they went to extremes and innovated, so they strayed from the straight path

(And We have already sent Noah and Abraham and placed in their descendants prophethood and scripture; and among them is he who is guided, but many of them are defiantly disobedient (26) Then We sent following their footsteps Our messengers and followed them with Jesus, the son of Mary, and gave him the Gospel. And We placed in the hearts of those who followed him compassion and mercy and monasticism, which they innovated; We did not prescribe it for them except for seeking the approval of Allah, but they did not observe it with due observance. So We gave the ones who believed among them their reward, but many of them are defiantly disobedient (27) (Al-Hadid)

So the believer does not resemble the People of the Book even in their states of heart: he is not hard-hearted like the Jews, nor a monk like the Christians, nor like them in excessive softness to the point of neglecting the prescribed punishments and not establishing justice among people. And He said in the verse of the prescribed punishment (and do not be taken by pity for them in the religion of Allah, if you should believe in Allah and the Last Day (2) (An-Nur)

Do not be like the Christians, for whom religion is love without fear, tolerance without limit, and tenderness that led them to make the forbidden lawful, so wrongdoing spread among them because they neglected the prescribed limits.

(and monasticism, which they innovated; We did not prescribe it for them except for seeking the approval of Allah, but they did not observe it with due observance) And the meaning may be: they did not observe what Allah obligated upon them, so how would they observe what they obligated upon themselves?

Whoever's self is not upright upon the command of Allah and His messengers cannot make it upright by himself, for he is more incapable of that and weaker.

And whoever surrenders himself to Allah and His Messenger ﷺ and obeys them without adding or subtracting in following, it is a right upon Allah to guide him to what is most upright, to grant him victory over his whims, and to raise him above himself.

And that the Christians, even when they innovated, did not follow! So they are neither those who followed Allah and the messengers, nor those who stayed upright upon what they innovated; so how would Allah guide the defiantly disobedient people? And that the Christians did not observe the approval of Allah in His Oneness, so they disbelieved and said that Allah is the Messiah, son of Mary (and He does not approve for His servants disbelief); had they observed His approval, they would not have disbelieved. And this is a rebuttal to the innovating Sufis who claimed love and went to extremes in it, (Say, "If you should love Allah, then follow me, Allah will love you") if you truly claim divine love, then why did you turn away from the Muhammadan guidance and innovate words of remembrance and sayings for which Allah sent down no authority, and contrive states that have no place in Islam?!

(O you who have believed, fear Allah and believe in His Messenger; He will give you a double portion of His mercy and make for you a light by which you will walk and forgive you; and Allah is Forgiving and Merciful (28) (Al-Hadid)

Throughout Surat al-Hadid (believe in Allah and His Messenger), except here He said (fear Allah) by being mindful of Him and in awe of Him unseen, (He will give you a double portion of His mercy and make for you a light by which you will walk) the reward is of the same kind as the deed: since they were mindful of Allah in all their states, He made for them a light like one walking with them, accompanying them everywhere, directing them, granting them success, helping them and giving them victory through it; so mercy is above the servant and light at his sides, like a companion who does not leave whoever has kept company with the Quran. So how could the devils of mankind and jinn, after this solid fortification, have any authority over one who has such protection and care from the Lord?!

So the believer who fortified himself with the light of the Quran and was mindful of Allah in his private and public life has toiled in mindfulness and taken pains to keep this state of being mindful of Allah and knowing that He sees everywhere (and He is with you wherever you are), and the reward is that a double portion of mercy accompanies him, and light, everywhere

And the verse from Surat Al-An'am explains it (And is one who was dead and We gave him life and made for him light by which to walk among the people like one who is in darkness, never emerging therefrom? (122)

He was dead and He gave him life with the Quran (al-Tafsir al-Muharrar), and reviving the heart from heedlessness is part of Allah's mercy (So observe the effects of the mercy of Allah — how He gives life to the earth after its death) (Ar-Rum), so it corresponds to His saying (He will give you a double portion of His mercy)

(and made for him light by which to walk among the people) corresponds to (and make for you a light by which you will walk)

So the meaning here in Al-Hadid is a light by which you walk within yourselves and among people

"He mentioned the granting of light to the dead and said: by which to walk among the people, meaning: it accompanies him however he moves about; and He said: among the people, as an indication of its illuminating him himself and others among the people, so He mentioned that the believer's benefit is not confined to himself" (al-Tafsir al-Muharrar - from the tafsir of Al-An'am)

So that the People of the Scripture may know that they are not able to obtain anything from the bounty of Allah, and that bounty is in the hand of Allah; He gives it to whom He wills, and Allah is the possessor of great bounty (29)

He began the surah with the attributes and names of knowledge and power, and made clear their details throughout it: that He is able to revive hearts after their death through the Quran and soften them with humility, and that humility comes from being mindful of Allah and knowing that He is with the servant (and He is with you wherever you are), and also from not being deluded by the world so that it does not distract him from the remembrance of Allah and his daily portion of the Quran; and He closed it with this knowledge: that the bounty of guidance and the Book is in the hand of Allah, He gives it to whom He wills and withholds it from whom He wills, because He is the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, and the possessor of great bounty.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
