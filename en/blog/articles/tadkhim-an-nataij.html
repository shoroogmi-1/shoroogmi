Magnifying Bad Outcomes

  • 📅 September 28, 2026
  • ⏱️ 2 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

It is in the very nature of human perception to magnify the negative outcomes of the bad experiences that befall us.

And that is one enormous psychological trap!

If you knew that this very cast of mind lay behind the collapse of giant companies, you would never again take lightly this nature you already know so well.

Okay?! So what's new? I know that out of dozens of compliments, we remember the one criticism we heard. I know.. thanks, I learned nothing

😳 Okay, bear with me!

Now, you can't change this nature.

- Understood!

But there are tactics you can put to work, and force yourself into, to break this perceptual habit: magnifying bad outcomes.

- What are they?

First- Just be aware of it: understand it, and notice it when it comes. That's all; don't do anything else. That alone is a huge step forward.

- Okay, next!

Second- Start a happiness file! Digital or paper, whichever you like, and gather into it the best news, the kindest comments, and the brightest moments of your life!

And whenever your brain, true to its nature, starts blowing up the negatives, make yourself, drag yourself if you have to, even when you don't feel like it, open that file and read something in it!

You'll find your sharp bad mood has broken, or let's say the blowing-up of negatives has dropped from 100% to 70–60%

What's happened is that you've broken that edge, that ugly magnification, and so afterward you'll make decisions more rationally; you won't rush in, blind and unthinking, and make hard-line decisions on the strength of a sharp, soaring bad mood.

Always be on the lookout for some pleasant, positive technique that takes the edge off (an outing or a stroll, a chat with an old friend, jokes and banter with a colleague at work,, anything that lifts your mood) and make yourself do it even if you don't want to, and say: My dear self, I want what's good for you! You must look at this file, so that you don't make reckless decisions and hurt people who've done nothing wrong, all because of an ugly, soaring, razor-sharp magnification in your perception!

- Aha! Really useful

So glad 😌 And don't forget to like, share, and subscribe to the channel 😂
