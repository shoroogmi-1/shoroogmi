Our Lord, Give Us in This World [That Which Is] Good and in the Hereafter [That Which Is] Good

  • 📅 July 22, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

And among the people is he who says, "Our Lord, give us in this world," and he will have in the Hereafter no share: this is a warning to the believer not to be like the disbeliever, who asks for nothing but this world and has no faith in the Hereafter.

Instead, ask Allah for the good of this world and of the Hereafter: (But among them is he who says, "Our Lord, give us in this world [that which is] good and in the Hereafter [that which is] good and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.")

Those will have a share of what they have earned, and Allah is swift in account: that is, the believers who pray for the good of this world and the Hereafter will have their prayers answered: He gives them (a share: a portion), whether small or great, of what they have earned, meaning of what they prayed for. And Allah is swift in account: He hastens to answer your prayers, a reward for those who believed in the Hereafter and did not settle for this world alone.

"For if you ask Him, He will give you a share of what you asked for, in this world and in the Hereafter; and Allah hastens to answer your prayers." Ibn Ashur

— Shoroog Almehmadi
