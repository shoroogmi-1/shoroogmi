Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

Reflections on the Words of Allah the Exalted (even if their plan had been [sufficient] to do away with the mountains)

  • 📅 July 2, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

Whoever is targeted by plots, schemes and injustice, this itself points to his greatness, for Allah measured it against the mountain, the mightiest of His creations; and it is proof of the greatness of Muhammad ﷺ.

Whoever is targeted by plotting: the mountains give way, but he does not, so complete is Allah's protection of him, so perfect His care, so all-encompassing His knowledge of him

And it is proof that he stands firmly rooted in the land, however the heavens and the earth may scheme against him. For the mountains are firmly rooted in the land, yet they would give way if such plotting were aimed at them; while the righteous believer, once rooted in the land, stands firm as the mountains, and his mark is never wiped out or worn away (They want to extinguish the light of Allah with their mouths, but Allah refuses except to perfect His light, although the disbelievers dislike it)

Here is proof that Muhammad ﷺ is greater than the mountains in steadfastness and uprightness, more firmly rooted, longer in reach and in character (And indeed, you are of a great moral character).

— Shoroog Almehmadi
