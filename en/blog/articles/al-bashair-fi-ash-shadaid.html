Glad Tidings in Times of Hardship

  • 📅 September 10, 2026
  • ⏱️ 2 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

This gets spread around a lot: that glad tidings do not come while your heart is full of fear (And when the fright had left Ibrahim and the glad tidings had reached him)

How happy I am with anyone who reflects on the Quran, but sound reflection is built on a sound understanding of the Quran.

Because the meaning of the verse is: when Ibrahim (peace be upon him) sensed fear of his guests, then they told him that they were angels sent to the people of Lut, then they gave him glad tidings of Ishaq and Ya'qub (peace be upon him)

So the meaning is: the fright left him when they gave him glad tidings of Ishaq and Ya'qub.

And besides, there is no legal obligation in feelings; the believer is not held to account for them.

Rather, glad tidings come most often in times of hardship, fear, pain, and sorrow, and this is well known in the Quran, like the glad tidings to the Messenger ﷺ and the believers at a time of fear (the Battle of Badr) and reinforcing them with angels. For a person, at times of fear, is in greater need of glad tidings that steady his heart than at times of ease!

Read these verses carefully and don't let your eye skim past them quickly, so you understand that glad tidings come at a time of fear, to bind hearts and make feet firm through them:

(When you were calling on your Lord for help, and He answered you: I will reinforce you with a thousand of the angels, following one after another (9) And Allah made it only as glad tidings and so that your hearts would be reassured by it. And victory is only from Allah. Indeed, Allah is Mighty and Wise (10) When He covered you with drowsiness, a security from Him, and sent down upon you water from the sky to purify you with it, remove from you the defilement of Satan, bind your hearts, and make your feet firm with it (11) (Al-Anfal)

And if this understanding were sound, glad tidings would have been withheld from Ibrahim (peace be upon him); because he sensed fear of them !

You were afraid? Then no glad tidings for you!!

So this is an understanding that is not right.

And once again, let us read a concise tafsir and understand the verses, then write reflection and contemplation..

Of course, I am the one most in need of this advice. Our Lord, increase us in knowledge and understanding of the Quran.
