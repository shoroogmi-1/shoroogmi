Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

My Lord, Make Me Forget What Has Passed, and Help Me with What Remains

  • 📅 October 5, 2026
  • ⏱️ 2 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

When Qaylah (may Allah be pleased with her) lost her son, she wept before the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and told him of all the responsibilities his death had left on her shoulders:

“So I wept, then said: By Allah, O Messenger of Allah, I bore him a resolute man, and he fought alongside you on the day of al-Rabadhah, then he went off to bring me provisions from Khaybar, and its fever struck him, and he left the women in my care”

So he counseled her to supplicate, saying: “My Lord, make me forget what has passed, and help me with what remains” (a hasan hadith)

Here we learn that a person may ask Allah to make him forget the past and what he lost in it, and to help him through what is left of his life. And we learn that the best way to deal with a past that is gone is to forget it; and if he cannot forget, let him ask Allah to make him forget, and turn to the present and tend to it, and ask his Lord to help him carry the burdens and duties he has taken on — never imagining that help comes from anyone but Allah.

“By the One in whose hand is the soul of Muhammad, were you not a poor woman, we would have dragged you on your face today” — or “you would have been dragged on your face” (Abdullah was unsure) — “Is one of you unable to keep kindly company with his little companion in this world, and then, when one more entitled to him than he comes between them, to say: ‘Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return’?”

For Allah has more right than you to the one you lost.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
