The Righteous Friend

  • 📅 August 13, 2026
  • ⏱️ 3 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

The righteous friend wants you for your Lord, not for himself,

he seeks no advantage and asks you for no money; his whole bond with you turns on obedience: obey, and he draws you close; disobey, and he reproaches you; and if you will not stop, he keeps his distance. Know, then, that he is saving you for the Hereafter, and has no need of your worldly life.

He lifts your resolve toward the heights and walks there beside you when determination flags and aspiration falters; he prays for you, and his prayers for you are answered.

And in the Hereafter he will be with you on pulpits of light, for which the prophets and the martyrs will envy you.

Not every prophet has a righteous friend; some of them will be raised alone on the Day of Resurrection. And the friend intercedes for the believers, and for those he knew in this world, once Allah grants permission and is pleased with him,

and he is the anguish of the disbelievers, who say in the Fire, with a moan that tears at the heart, (So now we have no intercessors and not a devoted friend. Then if we only had a return [to the world] and could be of the believers): if only I were sent back to life and became a believer honored by Allah, so that I could intercede — or, at the very least, a righteous friend who knows me and whom I know, to intercede for me!

This provision comes from your Lord alone, so if you have been denied it, ask Him and call upon Him, and do not lose heart or grow weary: say, O Allah, grant me and make easy for me good and righteous companions, with whom I will sit on pulpits of light on the Day of Resurrection.

O people, listen and understand, and know that Allah has servants who are neither prophets nor martyrs, whom the prophets and the martyrs envy for their rank and their nearness to Allah. Then a Bedouin from the far edge of the gathering knelt down, gestured with his hand toward the Prophet ﷺ, and said: O Messenger of Allah: people among the people who are neither prophets nor martyrs, yet the prophets and the martyrs envy them for their places and their nearness [to Allah]? Describe them to us, portray them for us — that is, tell us what they are like. The Prophet's face lit up with joy at the Bedouin's question, and he said: They are people from among the obscure of humanity and strangers drawn from different tribes, bound by no close ties of kinship; they loved one another for the sake of Allah and were sincere toward one another. On the Day of Resurrection Allah will set up for them pulpits of light, and they will sit upon them, and He will make their faces light and their garments light. The people will be terrified on the Day of Resurrection, but they will not be terrified, and they are the allies of Allah: there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve. A hasan hadith

— Shoroog Almehmadi
