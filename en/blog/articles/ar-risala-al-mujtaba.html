Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

The Chosen Epistle on the Road to Being Chosen (Reflections on the Verses of Al Imran)

What Fits a Servant to Be Chosen

When their prophet told the Children of Israel that Talut had been raised up over them as king, they marvelled: how can kingship be his, when he is poor, with neither lineage nor wealth, and we are the ones descended from the line of kings!? So he told them that what fitted him to be chosen was "knowledge and power" (and has increased him abundantly in knowledge and stature). Knowledge comes first, for its surpassing honour; and stature is the strength that fits him for war, that is, any power or gift with which Allah has graced a person.

Here, though, we speak not of kingship, but of how Allah chooses His righteous servants to draw them near to Him, through the stations of servanthood.

When Allah chooses a servant, He does not ask him for ten years' experience! Nor for passion and eagerness, not at all! Were he drowning in darkness, the will of Allah is above the will of every creature, even of one who resists guidance and hates it. And yet there are qualities to be seen in those whom Allah chooses:

Humility: before the truth, and surrender to it; for Allah does not guide the arrogant (I will turn away from My signs those who are arrogant upon the earth without right).

Praying for guidance: that He make him righteous and admit him among the righteous

A love of knowledge, a search for the truth, earnestness and hard work, in any knowledge at all! For Allah loves resolve, and holding the books with strength; and this is what fits a person, tomorrow, to study His Mighty Book.

Power and talent in anything: so that tomorrow he may be a hope that strengthens his society and the homeland he lives in, once he has become righteous, and then a setter-right of others.

The Surah of the Righteous

(Say, [O Muhammad], "If you should love Allah, then follow me, [so] Allah will love you and forgive you your sins. And Allah is Forgiving and Merciful.") The road to love lies in these two, the Quran and the Sunnah: whoever follows them and lives by them, his face has shone with success.

Then He shows that the road to Allah's love has its landmarks and its stars, in whose footsteps a person walks until he arrives:

(Indeed, Allah chose Adam and Noah and the family of Abraham and the family of 'Imran over the worlds. Descendants, some of them from others. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing.) I pondered it: Adam and Noah are named alone, but with Abraham it is his household that is named, and likewise the family of 'Imran, because their offspring were righteous, a house that shone with light. In it, the surah lays out the conduct and the road of the friends of Allah, so that we may follow their example on the path of righteousness and nearness to Him; that is why it is named Al Imran; and for the rank of Abraham (peace be upon him), he is mentioned often in this surah.

And Allah is Hearing and Knowing: "Allah knows who deserves to be chosen, and chooses him; for He hears their words, and knows their states and what lies within them; and so He favoured them and chose them... This points to His having chosen them with complete knowledge of them, so as to make their states desirable and to encourage following their deeds and words." al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

(from among the righteous) shows that there is no rank greater than that of being righteous; for the righteous one, in all he does and all he leaves undone, holds fast to the most fitting way and the most perfect path; and this, as is known, takes in every station of this world and of religion, in the acts of the heart and the acts of the limbs" al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

The road begins with guidance, and next comes good tidings (guidance and good tidings)

Then, once his foot stands firm in guidance,

the good tidings reach him halfway!

(So the angels called him while he was standing in prayer in the chamber, "Indeed, Allah gives you good tidings") openings hastened to the one who hastens to good

And the foot stands firm only through knowledge

(But those firm in knowledge)

“Whoever keeps his oath, whose tongue is truthful, whose heart is upright, and who keeps his belly and his private parts chaste: that is the one firm in knowledge”

And al-Fakhr said: he is the one who knows Allah, and who knows the Quran. In sum: to be firm in knowledge is not a matter of many books and much memorized, but of living by the Quran and studying it, knowing Allah, and striving in earnest, in deed and in learning, along this road.

An upright heart!

(O Mary, indeed Allah has chosen you and purified you and chosen you above the women of the worlds)

The ranks of being chosen:

1- Chosen within oneself: by being purified of faults and sins (indeed Allah has chosen you)

2- Chosen above others: (and chosen you above the women of the worlds).

"He chose her for her abundant worship, her renunciation, her nobility, and her purity from all that sullies and from evil whisperings" - Ibn Kathir

And so too John (peace be upon him) (honorable, abstaining [from women]) ḥaṣūran: one who holds himself back from his desires; and he reached this station through constant striving.

And whoever neglects that striving will know much torment of the soul! (You are free: yours to choose, the pain or the cure)

(Before, as guidance for the people. And He revealed the Qur'an. Indeed, those who disbelieve in the verses of Allah will have a severe punishment): "in it, what befalls the soul of the narrowness and pain of punishment is likened to one bound tight, fettered, hemmed in." -al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

And the one who cares for the Quran, who lives by it and recites it, dwells in healing and in a garden

(Beautified for people is the love of that which they desire) He made them beautiful in the soul, as a test; but for them, the hypocrite could not be told from the believer, nor the believer from the God-fearing, nor the God-fearing from the purest, who puts nothing before the love of Allah

In al-Bukhari, Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) said: (O Allah, we cannot help but rejoice in what You have made beautiful for us; O Allah, I ask You that I may spend it as it ought to be spent). "Allah's making beautiful means: by bringing these things into being, making them ready to be enjoyed, and shaping human nature to lean toward them. Allah the Exalted makes them beautiful as a trial and a test; for were these things not made beautiful in people's hearts, the true believer would never be known. The one strong in faith does not put them before the love of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic." al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

To stand guard at the frontiers of the soul is the greatest ribat of all: keeping watch over imaginings and memories, lest their scent and their stirring slip through to the heart, and driving them off by seeking refuge in Allah and declaring that there is no god but Him

And Al Imran closes on this very meaning (O you who have believed, persevere and endure and remain stationed and fear Allah that you may be successful)

Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) gave the occasion of this verse's revelation: "There was, in the time of the Prophet ﷺ, no campaign in which they kept guard; rather, it was revealed about people who fill the mosques with life, praying the prayers at their times and then remembering Allah in them: (persevere) in the five prayers, (and endure) against yourselves and your desires, (and remain stationed) in your mosques." - Ibn Kathir

“Performing Wudu well in difficulty, and taking many steps to the Masajid, and waiting for Salat after Salat, That is the Ribat …and he repeated it three times”

Whether you show what is within yourselves..: "a call to purify the heart and to keep Allah's knowledge present at every moment, so that the servant is ashamed for his Lord to see his heart a home for every vile thought, and busies his mind instead with what draws him near to Allah… what is in your breasts: the inner selves and what they hide; this follows the familiar usage of the language, which ascribes the soul's passing thoughts to the breast and the heart, because the soul's emotions and the wavering of thought are all felt as stirrings in the breast" al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

(and caused her to grow in a good manner and put her in the care of Zechariah)

Mary (peace be upon her) was in Zechariah's care, and this was Allah causing her to grow in a good manner: He set before her an example to follow in righteous deeds.

So whoever seeks the road of guidance, let him watch the righteous and keep their company, bind himself to them and seek them out, listen and read, in the hope that a spark of their blessing may fall to him.

The Blessings of Being Denied

(Never will you attain the good [reward] until you spend [in the way of Allah] from that which you love), and after it comes (All food was lawful to the Children of Israel except what Israel had made unlawful to himself): Israel (that is, Jacob) forbade himself camel meat because of the sciatica he suffered.

How the two verses fit together:

You will attain no good, and the road of guidance is one, except by two things:

1- That you spend some of what you love (He said: from that which you love, which is His mercy to us: some of it, not all of it), and He set no limit on it: spending covers everything Allah has given you: time, knowledge, wealth...

So you will not attain the knowledge of the Quran and the understanding of it, nor reach the summit and the truth of living by it, until you give it all your time, so that it may give you some of itself

2- Denial: denying yourself what you love, crave and long for. And among these are your passions: hold yourself back from them and hem them in (abstaining [from women]), as though laying siege to your desires; and when the winds of denial blow, the breezes of gifts follow. This is what Ibn Ata'illah meant: (the withholding was the very essence of the giving).

(So those who emigrated or were evicted from their homes)

The emigration of the friends of Allah is an emigration from evil: from evil deeds, from evil company, and from all that is beneath people of honour. “The emigrant is the one who forsakes evil.” “A Muhajir (emigrant) is the one who gives up (abandons) all what Allah has forbidden.” These are the ones whose prayer Allah answered (Our Lord, and grant us what You promised us through Your messengers..): they longed for victory in this world, and He gave them word that their prayer was answered (And their Lord responded to them..), because of their emigration, their patience and their striving.

(distinguished in this world and the Hereafter)

Distinction in this world comes through knowledge of the religion. Al-Shafi'i said: "Allah has never taken an ignorant man as His friend; if He takes him, He teaches him."

For worship alone, without knowledge, may make the feet sink where they should stand firm; Satan dresses it up for its doer, who may then fall into excess and other ills. So the friend of Allah must have beneficial knowledge that he learns and teaches, and righteous deeds that draw him near.

he found with her provision: books and pages of learning. For knowledge is the provision of the one who knows Allah, and his daily bread.

(They are not [all] the same; among the People of the Scripture is a community standing [in obedience], reciting the verses of Allah during periods of the night and prostrating [in prayer]) They are those among the People of the Scripture who entered Islam; they stood in prayer by night while others slept. We cannot imagine a friend of Allah who never prays at night, or the witr, even a single rak'ah; and we cannot imagine a friend of Allah without a daily portion of the Quran, even a single verse.

Gifts of Love and Graces of Nearness

He closes this road by showing that whoever is patient upon the path of Allah's friendship will, by Allah's grace, attain it (whoever walks the road arrives) ✨

And every kind of honour comes to him, the sweetness of openings, and victory over his enemies: "patience and God-consciousness drive enemies back, for He, glorified is He, says: And if you are patient and fear Allah, their plot will not harm you at all" al-Tafsir al-Muharrar

And victory in this world and the Hereafter (and make those who follow you [in submission to Allah alone] superior to those who disbelieve until the Day of Resurrection): they are the community of Muhammad ﷺ, who follow all the messengers, and for this Allah honours them with victory in this world and the Hereafter

But as for those who believed and did righteous deeds, He will give them in full their rewards ( "that is: as for those who believed and did righteous deeds, He will pay them their reward in full, with nothing lessened or withheld, in this world and the Hereafter: in this world through victory and triumph, honour, a good life, a good name and more besides; and in the Hereafter through Paradise and everlasting bliss" al-Tafsir al-Muharrar
