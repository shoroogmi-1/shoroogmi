Establishing Prayer Before Giving Zakah

  • 📅 July 13, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, placed the establishing of prayer before the giving of zakah in the Quran for a reason:

Prayer holds the Quran within it; when a person prays, the soul grows rich with the Quran, and so it is better able to enrich others through zakah and charity

And when Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani explained the hadith "Gabriel used to meet him every night in Ramadan to study the Holy Qur'an carefully together. Allah's Messenger ﷺ used to become more generous than the fast wind"

he commented: ( to study the Qur'an together ) It was said that the wisdom in this is that studying the Quran together renewed his bond with an ever greater richness of soul, and richness of soul is a cause of generosity."

— Shoroog Almehmadi
