Subtleties and Fine Points of Hajj (Surat al-Baqarah and Surat al-Hajj Side by Side)

  • 📅 August 8, 2026
  • ⏱️ 7 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

One of the wisdoms behind the rites Allah prescribed for Hajj is to remind us of the Day of Gathering. That is why He closed the verses on Hajj in Surat al-Baqarah with this very purpose:

(And remember Allah during [specific] numbered days. Then whoever hastens [his departure] in two days - there is no sin upon him; and whoever delays [until the third] - there is no sin upon him - for him who fears Allah. And fear Allah and know that unto Him you will be gathered)

And with it He opens Surat al-Hajj!

(O mankind, fear your Lord. Indeed, the convulsion of the [final] Hour is a terrible thing (1)

Al-Baqarah gives Hajj in brief; Surat al-Hajj gives it in full. So it dwells longer on the terrors of the Gathering (On the Day you see it every nursing mother will be distracted from that [child] she was nursing, and every pregnant woman will abort her pregnancy, and you will see the people [appearing] intoxicated while they are not intoxicated; but the punishment of Allah is severe (2) [al-Hajj]

And it sets out the rulings of Hajj in greater detail

And it answers those who deny the Resurrection (O People, if you should be in doubt about the Resurrection, then [consider that] indeed, We created you from dust..)

Facing trials

(And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge. If he is touched by good, he is reassured by it; but if he is struck by trial, he turns on his face [to the other direction]. He has lost [this] world and the Hereafter. That is what is the manifest loss (11) [al-Hajj]

And He gives an example of this turning back:

When Satan cast rhymed words upon the tongue of the Prophet ﷺ, Allah made that a trial to sift the believer from the hypocrite; and some did not hold firm, and went astray.

(And We did not send before you any messenger or prophet except that when he spoke [or recited], Satan threw into it [some misunderstanding]. But Allah abolishes that which Satan throws in; then Allah makes precise His verses. And Allah is Knowing and Wise (52)

[That is] so He may make what Satan throws in a trial for those within whose hearts is disease and those hard of heart. And indeed, the wrongdoers are in extreme dissension (53) And so those who were given knowledge may know that it is the truth from your Lord and [therefore] believe in it, and their hearts humbly submit to it. And indeed is Allah the Guide of those who have believed to a straight path (54) [al-Hajj]

He named the greatest cause of turning back on one’s heels: hypocrisy and hardness of heart; and in the verse that follows He named its cure: ikhbāt, humble submission, reverence, lowering oneself before Allah the Exalted (and their hearts humbly submit to it).

And He unfolds it further here:

(And for all religion We have appointed a rite [of sacrifice] that they may mention the name of Allah over what He has provided for them of [sacrificial] animals. For your god is one God, so to Him submit. And, [O Muhammad], give good tidings to the humble [before their Lord] (34) Who, when Allah is mentioned, their hearts are fearful, and [to] the patient over what has afflicted them, and the establishers of prayer and those who spend from what We have provided them (35) And the camels and cattle We have appointed for you as among the symbols of Allah; for you therein is good. So mention the name of Allah upon them when lined up [for sacrifice]; and when they are [lifeless] on their sides, then eat from them and feed the needy and the beggar. Thus have We subjected them to you that you may be grateful (36) [al-Hajj]

In Surat al-Baqarah He forbade quarreling and wickedness in word and deed: insults, curses, abuse, foolishness, anger, and lust.

Whoever strips himself of these traits will find the traits of ikhbāt from Surat al-Hajj settling in their place, for emptying comes before adorning: the trembling of the heart and its reverence when Allah is mentioned. That is why no one can be a quarreler without right, a habitual curser, and humbly reverent all at once! The two cannot meet.

And whoever gives up wickedness and quarreling, Allah repays him with light in his heart

“There is no gulp more beloved to Allah than a gulp of anger that a servant swallows; no servant swallows it for Allah’s sake but that Allah fills his inside with faith”

And when you give up quarreling with your brother, He repays you with light in your heart and a door flung open to understanding, a fitting reward; for the clear opening belongs to the one who understands the clear Book, and this is won only through the toil of practice and clenched teeth.

This is how I understand the hadith: whoever lets go of his anger, a door of understanding and wisdom is opened to him.

(and [to] the patient over what has afflicted them): their patience, their refusal to turn back on their heels when trials and hardships strike them (And of the people is he who worships Allah on an edge..) (al-Hajj:11)

And the establishers of prayer: everywhere else in the Quran it is wal-muqīmīna al-ṣalāh, except here in al-Hajj, where the nūn is dropped (wal-muqīmī al-ṣalāh); for the prayer is lightened for the pilgrim, who prays it combined and shortened —and Allah knows best—.

He lightened the prayer for the pilgrim and pressed the giving upon him; that is why He said (those who spend from what We have provided them), keeping the nūn in yunfiqūn; and the next verse makes clear what kind of spending is meant: the sacrificial offering of Hajj and the udhiyah.

(And the camels and cattle We have appointed for you as among the symbols of Allah; for you therein is good. So mention the name of Allah upon them when lined up [for sacrifice]; and when they are [lifeless] on their sides, then eat from them and feed the needy and the beggar. Thus have We subjected them to you that you may be grateful (36) [al-Hajj]

He knew that the one who gives up quarreling, absorbed in the Lord of Majesty and turned away from creation, does not keep watch over his enemies and what they weave behind his back. So He struck fear into those enemies, and reassured the humbly submissive that he should go on in his reverence and submission, and not break it off on their account, so great is the right of what he is engaged in, for Allah is enough for him. And so He said: (Indeed, Allah defends those who have believed. Indeed, Allah does not like everyone treacherous and ungrateful (38) [al-Hajj]

I reflected on Surat al-Baqarah and found that it begins with the verses of jihad and then Hajj, while Surat al-Hajj begins with Hajj and then jihad.

Perhaps the wisdom is that, in Allah’s foreknowledge, they would fight (the battles of Badr ,,), then would come the Conquest and the Hajj, and then jihad once more.

And how many a city did We destroy while it was committing wrong - so it is [now] fallen into ruin - and [how many] an abandoned well and [how many] a lofty palace (45) So have they not traveled through the earth and have hearts by which to reason and ears by which to hear? For indeed, it is not eyes that are blinded, but blinded are the hearts which are within the breasts (46)

There is a likeness between the land of the Gathering and the land of Hajj! Both stand bare of buildings

(and you will see the earth prominent, and We will gather them): that is, bare and white, with nothing on it and no building, a level plain.

So too the lands of the sacred sites, Arafah and Muzdalifah: no buildings stand on them.

And He mentions the ruined towns so that the pilgrim remembers all he has left behind him, home, land, palaces, and family, and that he has come to perform the rites alone, solitary, stripped of everything, bare in an empty land: and so will you be on the Day of Resurrection.

So too the Gathering comes after the terrors, after the splitting of the heavens, the constellations, and the moons; and so He mentions the ruined town, the abandoned well that no one draws from, and the lofty palace.

Then He mentions traveling through the land, so that the pilgrim, as he moves from one sacred site to the next: Mina-Arafah-Muzdalifah- Mina, remembers that this is how all creation will be journeying to Allah on the Day of Gathering; so let him have a heart by which to reason.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
