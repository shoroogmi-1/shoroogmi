The Righteous Dream

  • 📅 August 3, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

A righteous dream is a choosing from Allah the Exalted: He singles you out from among all His creation and lets you see it.

(And thus will your Lord choose you) Ibn Kathir (may Allah have mercy on him) said: "Yaqub is telling his son Yusuf: just as your Lord has chosen you, and shown you these stars, with the sun and the moon, prostrating to you.." so too will He teach you the interpretation of dreams and complete His favor upon you by making you a messenger and sending revelation to you.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
