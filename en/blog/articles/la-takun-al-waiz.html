With Your Children, Don't Be the Preacher

  • 📅 September 3, 2026
  • ⏱️ 1 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

With your children, don't be the preacher while they sit as your audience, as though you were Richard the war orator!

Instead, turn the sermon into a conversation you share: questions and answers, back-and-forth, a few brief words from you, and listening.

That is how the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was. Look at his hadiths and you will find that most of them are dialogues, though he could simply have poured his words into the listener's ear and walked on! But he knew that what we share and talk through together stays longer in the mind, and shows more respect for the person in front of us, who is a human being, not a wall to drone at. And question and answer also tells the one in front of you that you love to hear him speak.

"I was a companion rider of the Prophet ﷺ on a donkey. He said, 'O Mu'adh! Do you know what Allah's right on His slaves is, and what the right of His slaves on Him is?' I replied, 'Allah and His Apostle know better.' He said, 'Allah's right on His slaves is that they should worship Him (Alone) and should not worship any besides Him. And slave's right on Allah is that He should not punish him who worships none besides Him.'"

— Shoroog Almehmadi
