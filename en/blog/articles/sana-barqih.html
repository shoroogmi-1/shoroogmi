Reflections on the Quran & Sunnah

(The flash of its lightning almost takes away the sight)

  • 📅 August 19, 2026
  • ⏱️ 5 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

And the lightning in Surah Al-Baqarah is the proofs of the Quran, in His saying (The lightning almost snatches away their sight) (al-Tafsir al-Muharrar)

So the meaning of this verse in Surah An-Nur may be that the light of the Quran almost takes away the sight.

The Quran, when it is recited, is not a light on its own! Rather it is the light of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, upon the light of Jibril (peace be upon him), upon the light of Muhammad ﷺ, by the blessing of whose lips we received the Quran, upon the light of the angels who witness the Quran when it is recited, upon the light of the believer who recites it and listens to it (even if no fire touched it. Light upon light).

And the Quran's style is concision and ellipsis, so it will not say: light upon light upon light upon light upon light! That would weary the reader, so it sufficed with mentioning two lights, so that the reader would understand from them the rest of the complete lights.

Even if no fire touched it: even if he received no knowledge before it except the knowledge of the Quran, light would come to him, knowledge would be complete for him, and the stages of understanding and ascent would be perfected for him. Have you not seen your Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, who received no knowledge and no religious or cultural books before the Quran, yet surpassed the horizons with his knowledge!?

(And you are not in any matter, nor do you recite any of the Quran, nor do you do any deed, except that We are witnesses over you when you are engaged in it (61) (Yunus)

He made general every matter and deed, in that the angels preserve it, write it, and attend it; but as for the Quran, He singled it out by mention. And no, by Allah, He did not make every deed and matter general and single out the Quran apart from them except for the greatness of its rank and standing, and for the majesty of the Most Merciful and His angels witnessing it. Every matter and deed is for warning and awakening the sense of being watched, and the Quran is for encouragement and exalting its standing, for it is above every matter. And working with it and being occupied with it (reciting, listening, memorizing, explaining, and pondering) is the greatest of the witnessed deeds preserved in the record, and it is what makes the believer's face shine and his features light up when he is given his book in his right hand; his joy at what he finds of his occupation with the Quran (in the written record) is greater than his joy at the rest of his deeds, and it is the great news which, for its greatness, the creation of the heavens attend! Does this happen with a news bulletin? Or does it happen with ordinary conversations? No; these ordinary events are written by angels assigned to them (Allah knows best their number; in the Tafsir of Ibn Kathir their number is two, perhaps four, and it was said thirteen), but the Quran's standing is greater, for the angels witness it and call one another to it from every place, and place their wings upon the sides of the reciter, on the right and the left, and perhaps his shoulder, and an angel places his lips on the lips of the reciter: and this extraordinary thing does not happen with ordinary matters and the other goings-on of conversation

“When one of you gets up to pray at night, let him use the tooth-stick, for when one of you recites in his prayer, an angel places his mouth over his mouth, and nothing comes out of his mouth except that it enters the mouth of the angel” (its narrators are trustworthy)

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: “The most beloved speech to Allah is the recitation of the Quran; whoever sits down to recite it, the angels surround him with their wings, and mercy covers them, and they are the guests of the Most Merciful until they plunge into some other talk” (weak)

And reciting the Quran in a particular place brings blessing to it, makes mercy descend, and drives Satan out of it.

“Al-Nawawi said: He urged the voluntary prayer at home only because it is more hidden and further from showing off, and so that the house may be blessed by it, so mercy descends in it and Satan flees from it.”

This is the blessing of the Quran in a place, so how, by your Lord, about one who carries the Quran in a hidden place: the heart!

And this light is stronger than you imagine; its strength in driving away devils and subduing the self and its faults almost takes away the breaths and souls of the reciters, because of the majesty of Allah the Great, and because of the light of the angels.

“What is the matter with people who raise their eyes to the sky in their prayer … they will stop that, or their sight will be snatched away”

Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani (may Allah have mercy on him) explained this hadith, saying:

“And it was said the meaning is that it is feared for the eyes from the lights with which the angels descend upon those praying”

The light of the Quran almost takes away the sight of the hypocrites, and the devils among men and jinn, and it almost takes away the sight of the believers.

So if the strength of the Quran's light almost takes away keen, physical sight, then what about the darknesses and sins for which every means has become impossible and every trick has been exhausted in erasing them, removing them, and being protected from them? And how could the meaning not manifest itself in the heart of the one who recites it, almost taking away sights and physical things, until nothing remains in the place but light (Everyone upon it will perish).

Indeed, the light of the Quran is severe upon the devils, intense upon the heart, so intense that it takes away the darknesses and descends with light upon the servant, so his heart is illuminated, his deeds and words are light, and what is around him and his face are a shining moon.

— Shoroog Almehmadi
