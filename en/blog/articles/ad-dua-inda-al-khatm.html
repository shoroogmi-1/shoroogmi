It Is Established That Supplication Is Answered upon Completing the Quran

  • 📅 August 18, 2026
  • ⏱️ 2 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

“It is established from the great Tabi’i Mujahid ibn Jabr:

“Whoever completes the Quran is given a supplication that is not rejected.” And he also said: “It has reached us that supplication is answered upon completing the Quran.

Imam al-Bukhari said: “At every completion there is an answered supplication” (Dr. Abdulaziz al-Shaya)

“There are three whose supplication Allah does not reject: the one who remembers Allah much, the oppressed, and the just ruler” (hasan)

And the Quran is the greatest remembrance of all (Sad. By the Quran containing reminder). For if one says “Subhan Allah” alone, he receives one good deed for it, but if he recites it from the Quran, he receives ten good deeds for every letter, and a good deed is multiplied tenfold! (Dr. Ayman Suwayd)

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to stay up talking at night with Abu Bakr about some matter of the Muslims’ affairs, and one night he stayed up talking with him while I was with him. Then the Messenger of Allah ﷺ went out walking and we went out walking with him, and there was a man standing praying in the mosque. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ stood listening to his recitation, and when we were about to recognize the man, he said: “Whoever would be pleased to recite the Quran fresh, as it was revealed, let him recite it according to the recitation of Ibn Umm Abd.” He said: Then the man sat down to supplicate, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ began saying: “Ask and you will be given, ask and you will be given.”

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ prayed for whoever memorizes the Quran and the hadith and conveys them, for radiance of the face and answered supplication.

“The Prophet ﷺ commanded the Ummah that whoever is present convey from him to whoever is absent, and he prayed for those who convey with an answered supplication … And he also said: “May Allah brighten a man who hears a hadith from us and conveys it to one who has not heard it, for perhaps a carrier of understanding is not one who understands, and perhaps a carrier of understanding conveys it to one who understands it better than he does; there are three things over which the heart of a Muslim does not harbor rancor: sincerity of deeds for Allah, sincere counsel to those in authority, and holding to the community of the Muslims; for their supplication surrounds those behind them, . And in this is a supplication from him for whoever conveys his hadith even if he is not a man of understanding, and a supplication for whoever conveys it even if the listener understands better than the one conveying; because of the radiance given to those who convey; and that is why Sufyan ibn Uyaynah said: You will not find anyone among the people of hadith except that there is radiance in his face; because of the supplication of the Prophet ﷺ”

Ibn Taymiyyah
