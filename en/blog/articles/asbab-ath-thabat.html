The Shoot: On the Means of Steadfastness in Guidance Until Death -by the Permission of Allah the Exalted-

  • 📅 August 12, 2026
  • ⏱️ 6 min read
  • 📖 … completed reads
  • ❤️ …

I know! You are apprehensive about swerving after uprightness; it is like a bee buzzing and buzzing in your head… When this thought comes to you, don't do anything and don't think too much, just say: "O Allah, Turner of hearts, make my heart firm upon Your religion."

And entrust guidance to its Owner, and hand the keys to its Guardian, and what an excellent Guardian He is.

Yes, steadfastness is from Allah the Exalted, and the servant must take its means #strive:

Don't sit with the people of falsehood who make you doubt the soundness of faith in Allah and the Last Day; don't listen to them, don't say I won't be affected; yes, you will be affected, and certainty in the Hereafter drops a degree or two, and then you barely manage to raise it again.

So are you prepared to raise it after every moment it drops!?

I'm not prepared!

Then don't listen to them.

If you come across lines that raise doubts about the foundations of the religion, don't play the expert and say (I'll confront it, let me read what's in it! What are these restrictions, I can refute it..)

For they have told us about someone who had memorized texts in fiqh and could reel them off, who exposed himself to the doubts of the atheists, and they stuck in his heart and would not leave, and he became an atheist after having been a proud monotheist -O Allah, we ask You for well-being-.

So, advice from the heart: don't confront it as long as you are not a specialist in this field.

I am not a specialist in refuting doubts; so why should I expose myself to them!?

Why, when the daylight is plain to him, does he ask like a fool: Does the sun exist or not?

Don't make your religion a playground for every passerby to mess with as he likes and ruin for you years of certainty that you built and worked hard on!

Have you seen a man who had wealth and standing, and then in a second demolished everything he had built and found it to be nothing! That is his likeness

(Whoever makes his religion a target for disputes will shift often)

A man came to al-Hasan al-Basri and said: O Abu Sa'id, I want to dispute with you. He said: (Keep away from me; for I know my religion; only one who doubts his religion disputes with you)

And Malik ibn Anas, when some of the people of desires came to him, would say: (As for me, I am upon clear evidence from my Lord, and as for you, you are doubtful, so go to a doubter like yourself and dispute with him) A man from the people of innovation came to him to debate him, and Malik said: (They confuse themselves, then seek someone to make things known to them) (Al-Aqidah al-Tahawiyyah - explanation by Prof. Dr. Khalid al-Muslih)

(See a previous article we published explaining the tafsir of the saying of Allah the Exalted (And recite to them the news of him to whom We gave Our signs, but he detached himself from them..)

And among its causes is deviation after guidance:

1- Giving precedence to reason over religious knowledge and faith.

2- Individual conceit and arrogance in accepting facts, evidence, and proofs.

3- Wavering in doubt without taking the means of certainty: among what increases certainty is religious knowledge, and learning the deeds and fiqh of the hearts to entrench faith and devotion to Allah, and humility toward the truth if it appears.

4- Being deluded by the adornment of the life of this world; keep the world in your hand (by taking the means and making the required effort) and don't keep it in your heart, so that you become attached to it and covetous of it; and this is the best definition of zuhd, and the sign of the heart's zuhd is that missing something does not sadden you nor does its coming make you rejoice; rather, a shortcoming in obedience saddens you and performing it makes you rejoice.

And the answer: No, you are not immune from being affected like the angels; you are that human being who affects and is affected.

So don't say: My mind can bear hearing stories and news, and watching pictures and videos, and is not affected by them; yes, your mind is affected without realizing it.

Don't say I love confrontation, not running away; for running away is sometimes the only way for a drowning person to be saved from drowning, and we have been commanded to flee from the lion: "Flee from the leper as you would flee from a lion." And how many a person's destruction lay in his courage {And do not kill yourselves}.

Have you not seen that the master of the brave, Muhammad, upon him be blessings and peace, was commanded to avoid sitting with the people of falsehood; and that while he was infallible! And he did not say I am a Prophet, I am not affected. {And when you see those who engage in mocking Our verses, then turn away from them until they engage in some other talk}.

Have you not seen that the Prophet and Messenger of Allah, Yusuf (peace be upon him), fled from the wife of al-Aziz {And they both raced to the door}.

And we were told about a righteous man who listened to the news and story of someone falling into a sin, and was affected by it most severely, and then committed the sin! Even though it was merely being told and hearing!

{And so that the hearts of those who do not believe in the Hereafter will incline toward it, and that they will be pleased with it, and that they will commit what they are committing} Dr. Ahmad Abdel-Moneim said: This verse mentions the stages of people's falling into fitnah -the steps of being affected-:

1- Inclining (listening)

2- Being pleased with what they heard, without the heart rejecting what it heard

3- The act and the deed.

I say: According to the degree of faith in Allah and the unseen is the strength of the heart in the face of hearing fitnah and its firmness in the face of doubt and desire; and however far faith reaches, one must flee and not listen.

Have you not seen that the heroes and champions of war are commanded to lower their gaze! Is lowering the gaze not a form of running away!

And the question for this was: Can a person resist being influenced?

— Shoroog Almehmadi
